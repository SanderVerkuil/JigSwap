# JigSwap market scan: comparables, feature patterns, pain points (as of Oct 2026)

**Method and caveats.** I ran web searches and fetched pages. App-store listings and product pages describe the vendors' own claims, and some figures come from secondary sources, which are flagged. To make the gap columns accurate, I checked what JigSwap already has by reading `/home/sander/Documenten/Projects/SanderVerkuil/JigSwap/packages/backend/convex/schema.ts` and grepping `apps/web/src`. That baseline comes from the schema and grep only, so confirm it against the live UI.

**What JigSwap already has (from the repo):**
- EAN/UPC fields on puzzles (schema.ts ~L175) and import from a product-page URL (`packages/backend/convex/catalog/importCache.ts`).
- A 5-step condition scale and `missingPiecesCount` (~L290-299), plus a per-solve `allPiecesPresent` flag and a `wasBorrowed` snapshot (~L508-525).
- Flags for trade, sale and lend.
- Exchanges of type trade, sale or loan, with both sides confirming receipt and a `disputed` status (~L810).
- Open-ended loans with an expected return date and a recall option (~L1866).
- An ownership chain-of-custody log, `copyCustodyEntries` (~L1854).
- Partner reviews with four sub-scores (communication, packaging, condition, timeliness) and a credibility score (~L1205-1244).
- Wishlists, goals, households, circles, follows and messaging.
- Reports with reasons and moderation.

**Not found in the repo:**
- Camera barcode scanning (no `BarcodeDetector`, `getUserMedia` or ZXing in `apps/web/src`).
- A live stopwatch. Solve duration is typed in as minutes in `apps/web/src/components/solving/finish-solve-dialog.tsx`.
- A notification when a wishlist puzzle becomes available.
- A shipping or hand-off method. It was explicitly dropped, see `packages/backend/convex/exchange/propose.ts:98`.
- Events.

## 2. Feature patterns worth adopting (25)

Status means what JigSwap has today, based on the repo baseline above.

1. **Camera EAN scanning, including bulk scanning, to add or look up puzzles.** Puzzle Tracker, Hey Puzzlers, MySpeedPuzzling, Jvh Puzzle and Libib all have it (sources above). *Why:* it is the expected way in, and JigSwap already stores EANs. *PWA catch:* `BarcodeDetector` works on Chrome for Android but not on iOS Safari (behind a flag) or Firefox, so you need a ZXing/WASM fallback (https://caniuse.com/mdn-api_barcodedetector ; https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/barcode-detector-api-polyfill@1.2.0/README.md). *Status:* missing.
2. **"You already own this" warning when adding or scanning.** Puzzle Tracker's duplicate detection, and reviewers praise avoiding duplicate buys (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.puzzletimer ; https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puzzle-tracker/id1561473799). Libib does the same (https://detourdetroiter.com/heres-a-fast-and-satisfying-way-to-organize-your-books). *Why:* buying duplicates is a real collector pain, and the warning is even more useful across a household. *Status:* not verified.
3. **A chain of metadata sources, tried in order.** Retailer and brand pages carry EANs (https://www.ravensburger.co.uk/en-GB/products/jigsaw-puzzles/adult-puzzles/puzzle-community-12001739). Open Icecat has toys datasheets with EANs, including Jumbo puzzles (https://icecat.co.uk/menu/channelpartners ; https://icecat.fr/amp/p/vendorName/mpn/desc-34633085.html). Open Products Facts is open data but has gaps (https://cz-en.openproductsfacts.org/). *Why:* fewer empty records. *Status:* partial; URL import exists (`importCache.ts`).
4. **Crowd corrections with contributor recognition.** IPDb has error reporting and contributor lists (https://ipdb.tawk.help/article/about-ipdb). *Why:* catalogue quality decides whether volunteers keep contributing. *Status:* partial; suggest-edit proposals exist, recognition does not.
5. **A lifecycle or "disposition" status for each copy** (keep, to swap, lent, given away, sold, abandoned). Puzzle Tracker users asked for a "disposition" field and the app added an Abandoned folder (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puzzle-tracker/id1561473799). A whole puzzling guide is organised around disposition (https://sites.google.com/view/itspuzzling/disposition/dont-keep/trade). *Status:* partial (trade/sale/lend flags, `heldBy`).
6. **Saying where pieces are missing, not just how many.** Libraries keep a missing-piece card in the box (https://bpl.on.ca/news/puzzles). Reddit swappers circle the gap on the box picture (https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rkunzb/snapshots/2026-03-06T23%3A15%3A49.175883Z). *Why:* you can only build trust in a puzzle's completeness if the disclosure is specific and dated. *Status:* partial (count plus per-solve flag); add a marker on the box photo and a per-borrower history.
7. **Matching lost and extra pieces between owners of the same puzzle.** The NZ group reunited a piece with its owner by chance (https://www.rnz.co.nz/life/lifestyle/a-christmas-miracle-in-new-zealand-s-puzzle-community). Ravensburger does not replace single pieces for puzzles under 9,000 pieces in the UK and US (https://ravensburger-en.mindtouch.us/Service_and_Support/Faulty_Items/Missing_a_puzzle_piece_(5000_pieces_or_less_puzzles) ; https://ravensburger.us/en-US/service/care-commitment). *Why:* JigSwap knows who owns the same puzzle, so it can route "found an extra piece" posts to them. Nobody else offers this. *Status:* missing.
8. **Completeness confidence levels** (assembled, weighed, unverified). Weighing a sample of pieces gives a rough count but is noisy and cannot spot foreign pieces (https://community.ebay.co.uk/t5/Family-Home-and-Garden/Jigsaw-Question/m-p/3066429 ; https://lists.oplin.org/pipermail/oplinlist/2025-September/026311.html). Marktplaats sellers write "very likely complete, can't guarantee" (https://www.marktplaats.nl/v/hobby-en-vrije-tijd/denksport-en-puzzels/m2424065598-24-puzzels-te-koop-jan-van-haasteren-wasgij). *Status:* partial; this could be derived from the last solve with `allPiecesPresent`.
9. **Separate grades for the box and the pieces.** Discogs grades the record and the sleeve separately (https://milehighvinyl.ca/pages/news). *Why:* a crushed box with perfect pieces is common and should be graded differently from damaged pieces. *Status:* a single 5-step condition.
10. **Hygiene and allergy flags** (smoke-free, pet-free home). Library swaps require or ask for disclosure of these (https://rapidcitylibrary.org/event/10797469 ; https://pekinpubliclibrary.libcal.com/event/15475798 ; https://delawarelibraries.libcal.com/event/15493734 ; https://www.schlowlibrary.org/events/3573). *Status:* missing.
11. **Choosing hand-off mode: local pickup or post, ship-to countries, who pays postage.** MySpeedPuzzling profiles set ship-to countries and costs (https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/marketplace/how-it-works). In Facebook swaps each side pays its own postage (https://sites.google.com/view/itspuzzling/disposition/dont-keep/trade). Jersey swaps happen in car parks and cafés (https://www.bailiwickexpress.com/news/jigsaw-swap-allows-islanders-share-hobby/). The Dutch letterbox-parcel limit is 38×26.5×3.2 cm (https://www.myparcel.nl/app/uploads/myparcel-vervoerdersspecificaties-2026-nl.pdf); my inference is that most puzzle boxes are deeper, so posting one costs a full parcel. *Status:* explicitly dropped (`propose.ts:98`).
12. **Reserve, settle, then the copy moves to the new owner.** MySpeedPuzzling does this (https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/marketplace/how-it-works). *Status:* JigSwap already matches this with accepted/completed, two-sided confirmation and custody entries.
13. **Two-way reputation with sub-scores and blocking.** MySpeedPuzzling has ratings and a block option. Discogs users build tools that filter sellers by rating (https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/discogs-notifier/dehhbkcndiodanfodegcgibmidgoekbj). *Status:* JigSwap is stronger here (four sub-scores plus credibility). Consider showing the number of completed swaps.
14. **Lightweight trust for loans instead of deposits.** Peerby's €2,000 guarantee (https://apps.apple.com/nl/app/peerby/id611218631) was not enough for its members, so Peerby moved to a paid, commission model (https://mtsprout.nl/leiderschap/management/oprichter-peerby-wil-je-innoveren-ga-klantonderzoek-doen). Libraries charge repair costs (https://huntingtonpub.lib.in.us/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/library-of-things-20250115.pdf). *Why:* puzzles are cheap, so social trust (circles, households) plus overdue reminders and a "swap-as-collateral" option fits better than handling money. *Status:* loans with expected return exist; reminders and collateral do not.
15. **Alerts when a wishlist puzzle becomes available, and two-way trade matches.** Discogs Wantlister sends real-time or digest alerts (https://support.discogs.com/hc/fr/articles/26355982507149-Comment-fonctionne-la-Wantlister). Hoefnagel ships from a wishlist queue (https://www.artifactpuzzles.com/products/6-month-puzzle-club-subscription). *Status:* wishlists exist; there is no match notification type in the schema.
16. **Swap-fair and event support.** Pre-list what you are bringing, issue a ticket or credit per puzzle, and enforce rules such as complete only, bagged, or like-for-like for a brand. Obdam (https://www.rodi.nl/koggenland/nieuws/483583/grote-puzzelruilbeurs-in-obdam), ticket-based library swaps (https://rapidcitylibrary.org/event/10797469) and MySpeedPuzzling's free organiser tools (https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/for-organizers) all show demand. *Status:* missing.
17. **A live timer with pause and sessions, plus solve-type metadata** (solo, pair or team; first attempt). Puzzle Tracker has sessions with pause (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puzzle-tracker/id1561473799). MySpeedPuzzling's stopwatch is free (https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/faq). WJPC uses timed rounds with sealed puzzles (https://worldjigsawpuzzle.org/wjpc/2025/rules). *Status:* duration is entered manually in minutes.
18. **Challenges, badges and yearly recaps.** The Goodreads challenge shows progress to friends (https://www.goodreads.com/blog/show/1130 ; https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2012.03932). IPDb has a monthly badge (https://ipdb.tawk.help/article/about-ipdb). StoryGraph has recaps (https://reedsy.com/discovery/blog/goodreads-vs-storygraph). My inference: daily streaks fit multi-day puzzles badly, so weekly or monthly cadences are safer. *Status:* personal goals exist; sharing them in circles and recaps does not.
19. **A "what should I do next?" picker.** Puzzle Tracker's wheel (users want to filter it by tags), MySpeedPuzzling's suggestion feature and IPDb's picker (sources above). *Status:* missing.
20. **A puzzle "passport": printable QR label and a public journey page.** BookCrossing has ID labels and journals (https://www.bookcrossing.com/about). Jigsaw Passport and Zurich University run travelling puzzles (https://www.skool.com/jigsaw-passport-6219/about ; https://www.vatp.uzh.ch/en/atp-kampagne/puzzle.html). MySpeedPuzzling mentions QR codes on puzzles (https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/faq). *Status:* partial; the custody log covers ownership changes only, so extend it to loans and borrowers' solves.
21. **Several people sharing one collection (households).** A Puzzle Tracker review asks for a "second profile" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puzzle-tracker/id1561473799). *Status:* JigSwap already has this; market it.
22. **Library or club mode for circles** (holds queue, default loan length, honour-system returns). Burlington and Greenburgh run 3-week loans (https://bpl.on.ca/news/puzzles ; https://greenburghlibrary.org/puzzles); Perth & Kinross runs on honour (https://culturepk.org.uk/libraries/services-and-programs/jigsawexchange/); Libib has patron lending (https://support.libib.com/libib/mobile-app/lending/lending.html). *Why:* care homes, libraries and clubs become channels for growth. *Status:* missing.
23. **Accessibility for older and less confident users.** NN/g reports that seniors are slower, have lower task success, struggle with small targets and nested menus, and are tripped up by strict form input (https://www.nngroup.com/videos/changes-seniors-computers/ ; https://informationgift.com/2002/04/web_usability_for_senior_citiz.html ; https://econsultancy.com/six-design-tips-for-making-your-website-senior-friendly/). Puzzle Tracker offers font sizing. *Premise check:* the best data I found does not show that puzzlers are mostly 50+. US Ipsos 2019 data: 34% of 18-34s puzzle monthly versus 10% of 55+ (https://cardandpuzzle-website.pages.dev/blog/jigsaw-statistics/). NOS reports NK finalists are mostly young and highly educated (https://nos.nl/l/2224135). Older-skew claims I found were second-hand only (https://kaayee.com/blogs/puzzle-life/how-many-people-actually-do-jigsaw-puzzles). Design accessibly for everyone rather than as a separate "senior" mode.
24. **Messaging about cost and sustainability.** For example, a counter of "puzzles kept in circulation / € saved". Swap groups began over rising prices and finished puzzles sitting unused (https://westleedsdispatch.com/pudsey-jigsaw-swap-group-circulates-puzzles-throughout-town ; https://www.bailiwickexpress.com/news/jigsaw-swap-allows-islanders-share-hobby/). A Dutch puzzler: "Je maakt 'm toch maar één keer" (you only do it once) (https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/279233/puzzels-ruilen-in-coronatijd-blijkt-populair-je-maakt-m-toch-maar-een-keer). Ravensburger publishes sustainability claims such as dropping shrink-wrap for 89% less plastic (https://ravensburger-gruppe.de/en/responsibility/sustainability/index.html).
25. **Data export and a clear ownership story.** IPDb exports CSV/PDF (https://ipdb.tawk.help/article/about-ipdb). My Puzzle Cabinet is local-only (https://apps.apple.com/se/app/my-puzzle-cabinet/id6474093668). Goodreads users worry about Amazon owning their data (https://reedsy.com/discovery/blog/goodreads-vs-storygraph). Jigsaw Wiki was abandoned in 2021 (https://ipdb.tawk.help/article/about-ipdb). *Why:* EU hosting plus export is a credible trust signal. *Status:* not verified.

## 3. Top user pain points

1. **Missing pieces are the main source of distrust.**
   - A visitor to the Obdam fair recalls a swapped puzzle that turned out to be missing a piece (https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/341408/ruilbeurs-voor-puzzels-in-obdam-drukbezocht-ik-heb-tien-nieuwe).
   - Libraries say they can't guarantee completeness (https://greenburghlibrary.org/puzzles).
   - Buyers use "missing piece" claims in eBay disputes (https://community.ebay.com/t5/Selling/A-Clever-Scam/m-p/33469508).
   - Manufacturers won't replace single pieces, and Trustpilot reviews mention refused claims (https://ravensburger-en.mindtouch.us/Service_and_Support/Faulty_Items/Missing_a_puzzle_piece_(5000_pieces_or_less_puzzles) ; https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.ravensburger.co.uk).
2. **Buying duplicates and losing track of the collection** (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/puzzle-tracker/id1561473799).
3. **Finished puzzles pile up because they are done once** (https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/279233/puzzels-ruilen-in-coronatijd-blijkt-populair-je-maakt-m-toch-maar-een-keer ; https://www.bailiwickexpress.com/news/jigsaw-swap-allows-islanders-share-hobby/ ; https://boards.straightdope.com/t/what-do-you-do-with-jigsaw-puzzles-youve-finished/728528).

## 4. Regulatory notes (brief; not legal advice)

- **DSA.** Hosting duties apply at any size:
  - notice-and-action with confirmation of receipt;
  - a statement of reasons for any restriction, including redress options;
  - points of contact and clear terms;
  - notifying police of threats to life.

  Micro and small enterprises are exempt from transparency reporting and from the extra platform duties (complaint handling, trader traceability). They must still report monthly active users on request. Sources: https://traficom.fi/en/data-economy-and-data-intermediation/hosting-services ; https://trust.dev.doctena.com/dsa ; https://blog.beuc.eu/?p=2641 ; https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/document-summary?id=1690454.

  JigSwap's `reports` table and the `report_decided` notification line up with this. Check that receipt confirmations and redress text are actually sent.
- **DAC7.** Because JigSwap supports sales with a `salePrice`, check whether it counts as a reporting platform operator. Sellers of goods are exempt below 30 sales and €2,000 a year; the Belastingdienst's wording of that test differs from other sources. Paid rentals have no exemption, which matters if paid lending is ever added. Sources: https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/en/business/content/information-for-sellers-dac7 ; https://vivid.money/en-de/blog/dac7/ ; https://www.grantthornton.ie/insights/factsheets/dac-7-reporting-obligations-for-digital-platforms/.
- **GDPR.** Under the legitimate-interest necessity test, prefer approximate location over GPS (https://www.aoshearman.com/en/insights/ao-shearman-on-data/the-european-data-protection-board-publishes-draft-guidelines-on-legitimate-interest-under-gdpr). Strip EXIF GPS from solve and box photos taken at home (https://flaviocopes.com/exif-metadata-privacy/). JigSwap's image pipeline re-encodes uploads; check that this strips metadata.
- **EAA.** Service providers that are microenterprises are exempt. WCAG 2.1 AA (EN 301 549) is still the sensible target and becomes mandatory as JigSwap grows (https://askem.com/compliance/eaa/ ; https://koksal.av.tr/en/legislation/eu-accessibility-act-eaa-germanys-bfsg/).

**Strongest openings:**
- Proof of completeness: piece-location disclosure, completeness confidence levels, and lost-piece matching.
- A puzzle "passport" built on the custody log JigSwap already has.
- Wishlist-match alerts inside circles and households.
- Local hand-off and support for swap fairs.
- Dutch-first JvH and Wasgij catalogue coverage, which MySpeedPuzzling, the largest competitor, doesn't serve in Dutch.

**Main threat:** MySpeedPuzzling already combines a large EAN database, scanning, a marketplace and events.
