# FAQ & Troubleshooting

Tap a question to expand the answer.

<details>
<summary>I added a puzzle, but it's not showing in the public catalogue. Why?</summary>

New puzzle _definitions_ you contribute go to a moderation queue and must be **approved by an admin** before they appear publicly. Your own copy is still in your library — only the shared catalogue listing waits for approval. Until then it shows at the top of your Add-Puzzle chooser as an unapproved submission.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between a "puzzle" and a "copy"?</summary>

A **puzzle** (catalogue definition) is the product — title, brand, piece count, artwork. A **copy** is the specific physical puzzle _you_ own, with its own condition, photos, notes, and completion history. Multiple people can own copies of the same puzzle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I traded a puzzle away. Did I lose my solving history?</summary>

No. Your completion records and stats stay with **you**, not with the puzzle. The new owner gets a fresh history for their copy.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I see a puzzle I know someone owns?</summary>

Visibility is controlled by the owner. If a copy is **private** or only shared to a **friend circle** you're not in, it won't appear in Browse for you.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A puzzle I lent out isn't available for new exchanges.</summary>

That's expected — a copy that's on loan is automatically marked unavailable until it's returned. Use **Recall** (owner) or **Return** (borrower) to close the loan.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I complete an exchange? I clicked "Mark Complete" but nothing finalized.</summary>

Exchanges use **dual confirmation** — _both_ parties must confirm completion before ownership/possession transfers. Wait for your partner to also mark it complete.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I message this member?</summary>

You can message members you follow each other with, members in a friend circle
with you, and members you already have a conversation or exchange with.
Otherwise the **Message** button is disabled. See
[Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages#who-you-can-message).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I reply to a conversation?</summary>

When a conversation shows "You can't reply to this conversation.", the other
member is no longer reachable by you, for example because they blocked you. If
you blocked them yourself, the conversation offers **Unblock** instead. See
[Blocking & Reporting](/docs/discovering-and-community/blocking-and-reporting).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why is a photo in a conversation blurred?</summary>

Photos are blurred at first when the automatic check wasn't sure about them
(**Sensitive content**), when the check couldn't run (**Not checked**), or when
they come from someone you don't follow each other with (**You don't follow
each other**). Click **Show** to see
the photo.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The other member hasn't received my photo.</summary>

Photos appear in the conversation as soon as you send them, but they're
checked automatically first. Until the check is done you see **Checking
photo…** and the other member sees **Pending review**. If the check flags a
photo, it's removed and you can click **Ask for a review**. See
[Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages#photo-checks).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why didn't my invite link make us follow each other?</summary>

When someone signs up through your invite link, JigSwap asks them whether you
two should follow each other. You only follow each other once they choose
**Follow each other**, and you get a notification when they do. They have 30
days to answer. See
[People & Following](/docs/discovering-and-community/people-and-following#invite-friends).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't access the Admin area.</summary>

The Admin panel (categories management and puzzle moderation) is restricted to users with the **admin role**. Regular members don't have access.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I got signed out unexpectedly.</summary>

JigSwap keeps your session in sync; if you sign out in another tab or your session expires, you'll be returned to the sign-in screen. Sign back in and you'll resume where you were.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It says "A new version of JigSwap is available". What should I do?</summary>

We updated JigSwap while you had it open. Click **Reload** to switch to the
new version; finish anything you're typing first, because reloading clears
it. **Not now** hides the message until there's another update.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I export my data?</summary>

Go to **Insights** and use the **data export** button to download your personal data.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I change the app's language?</summary>

Use the language switcher to pick **English** or **Dutch**; your choice is saved automatically.

</details>
