# Households

A household lets the people you live with share one puzzle library. Everyone
in the household sees each other's puzzles in **My Puzzles**. Puzzles stay
owned by whoever added them, and solves stay personal.

**Where:** **My Library** → **Household** (`/household`).

## Create a household

1. Open **Household**.
2. Click **Create household**.

You become the household's **Admin**. You can be in one household at a time.

## Invite someone

Only the household admin can invite. The person joins only after they accept.

### Invite a member

1. On **Household**, under **Invite someone**, search members by name.
2. Click **Invite** next to the person.

They show as **Invited** under **Waiting for a response** until they answer.

### Invite with a link

Use a link to invite someone, for example a person who isn't on JigSwap yet.

1. On **Household**, under **Invite with a link**, click **Create invite link**.
2. Share the link. On devices that support it, **Share invite link** opens
   your device's share sheet. You can also use **Copy link**, **Email** or **WhatsApp**.

Each link works once and expires in 14 days. JigSwap doesn't see or store who
you send it to. To cancel links you haven't used yet, click
**Revoke open links**.

When someone opens the link, they can accept or decline the invitation. A new
member who signs up through the link finds the invitation on their
**Household** page. Nothing is shared until they accept.

## Accept or decline an invitation

1. Open the invitation notification, or go to **Household**. Invitations are
   listed under **Household invites**.
2. Click **Accept** or **Decline**.

## What household members can see and do

- Everyone sees everyone's puzzles in **My Puzzles**, labelled
  **Household · _name_**, including copies set to private.
- Any member can log their own solves on a household puzzle.
- What else a member may do with your puzzle depends on its
  **Household access**.

## Set household access for a puzzle

Each puzzle has one of two access levels:

- **Can manage** — housemates can edit, lend and swap it.
- **View only** — housemates can see it. In **My Puzzles** it is labelled
  **Household · _name_ · view only**.

Some actions stay with the owner whatever the access level: deleting the
puzzle, changing its household access, sharing it into a friend circle and
adding it to collections. Requests from other members for your puzzle still
come to you.

To set access for one puzzle:

1. Open the puzzle's page.
2. Under **Household access**, choose **Can manage**, **View only** or
   **Use household default**.

Only the owner can change a puzzle's household access.

## Change several puzzles at once

1. On **Household**, go to **My puzzles in this household**.
2. Optionally filter the list by household access.
3. Tick the puzzles you want to change, or use **Select all**.
4. Click **Set to Can manage**, **Set to View only** or
   **Use household default**.
5. Confirm with **Change access**.

You can change up to 200 puzzles at once. This list shows only your own
puzzles.

## Set the household default

The household default applies to every member's puzzles that have no access
setting of their own. A setting made for a specific puzzle always wins.

1. On **Household**, go to **Household settings**.
2. Under **Default access for puzzles**, choose **Can manage** or **View only**.
3. Confirm with **Change default**.

The page shows how many puzzles use the default. Only the household admin can
change it; other members see it read-only. A household starts with
**Can manage** as the default.

## Leave a household or remove a member

To leave, click **Leave household** and confirm. Your puzzles leave the
household with you, and you no longer see the others' puzzles.

The admin can **Remove** a member. Their puzzles leave the household with them.

If the admin leaves, the longest-standing member becomes the admin. If you're
the last member, the household is dissolved and any pending invites are
cancelled.
