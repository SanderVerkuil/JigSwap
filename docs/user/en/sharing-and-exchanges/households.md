# Households

A household lets the people you live with share one puzzle library. Everyone
in the household sees each other's puzzles in **My Puzzles**. Puzzles stay
owned by whoever added them, and solves stay personal.

**Where:** **My Library** → **Household** (`/household`). The page has three
tabs: **Members**, **My puzzles** and **Settings**.

## Create a household

1. Open **Household**.
2. Click **Create household**.

You become the household's **Admin**. You can be in one household at a time.

## Name your household

The admin can give the household a name, for example "The Smiths". It shows
at the top of **Household**. Only members of the household see it; people you
invite don't.

1. On **Household**, open the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Household name**, type a name of up to 100 characters.
3. Click **Save name**.

To remove the name, clear the field and click **Save name**. Other members see
the name on **Settings** but can't change it.

## Invite someone

Only the household admin can invite. The person joins only after they accept.

Click **Invite** at the top of **Household**. The dialog offers three ways to
invite: find a member, share a link, or send an email.

### Invite a member

1. Click **Invite**, then under **Find a member** search members by name.
2. Click **Invite** next to the person.

They show as **Invited** under **Waiting for a response** on the **Members**
tab until they answer.

### Invite with a link

Use a link to invite someone, for example a person who isn't on JigSwap yet.

1. Click **Invite**, then under **Invite with a link** click
   **Create invite link**.
2. Share the link. On devices that support it, **Share invite link** opens
   your device's share sheet. You can also use **Copy link**, **Email** or **WhatsApp**.

Each link works once and expires in 14 days. JigSwap doesn't see or store who
you send it to. To cancel links you haven't used yet, click
**Revoke open links**.

When someone opens the link, they can accept or decline the invitation. A new
member who signs up through the link finds the invitation on their
**Household** page. Nothing is shared until they accept.

### Invite by email

1. Click **Invite**, then under **Invite by email** enter the person's
   **Email address**.
2. Click **Invite by email**. You see **Invitation sent**.

They get one email, in your language, inviting them to join your household.
It's valid for 14 days. No reminders follow, and JigSwap keeps no copy of the
address. Under **Sent invitations** you see when each invitation was sent and
when it expires. To cancel one, click **Revoke** and confirm.

You can send up to 10 email invitations a day. An address gets at most one
invitation email every 30 days.

When they sign up through the email, the invitation waits on their
**Household** page. Nothing is shared until they accept, and joining doesn't
make anyone follow each other. If they already have an account, they don't
get an email: the invitation reaches them in JigSwap as a regular household
invite.

## Accept or decline an invitation

1. Open the invitation notification, or go to **Household**. Invitations are
   listed under **Household invites**.
2. Click **Accept** or **Decline**. If you'd rather start your own, click
   **Create household** under **Or start your own household**.

## Join another household

You can be in one household at a time. If you're already in one and someone
invites you to theirs, you can switch in one step:

1. Open the invitation notification and go to **Household**, or open the
   invite link. The invitation shows at the top of **Household**.
2. Click **Leave and join**.
3. Read what happens and confirm with **Leave and join**.

You leave your household and join theirs straight away. Your puzzles go with
you. If you were the only member, your old household is deleted and its open
invitations are cancelled. If you were its admin, the longest-standing member
becomes the admin. If anything stops the join (for example the invitation was
withdrawn), you stay in your household and nothing changes.

Not interested? Click **Decline**. Inviting someone never tells you whether
they're already in a household.

## What household members can see and do

- Everyone sees everyone's puzzles in **My Puzzles**, labelled
  **Household · _name_**, including copies set to private.
- Any member can log their own solves on a household puzzle.
- What else a member may do with your puzzle depends on its
  **Household access**.

## Set household access for a puzzle

Each puzzle has one of two access levels:

- **Can manage** — housemates can edit, lend and swap it.
- **View only** — housemates can see it. In **My Puzzles** it is labelled
  **Household · _name_ · view only**.

Some actions stay with the owner whatever the access level: deleting the
puzzle, changing its household access, sharing it into a friend circle and
adding it to collections. Requests from other members for your puzzle still
come to you.

To set access for one puzzle:

1. Open the puzzle's page.
2. Under **Household access**, choose **Can manage**, **View only** or
   **Use household default**.

Only the owner can change a puzzle's household access.

## Change several puzzles at once

1. On **Household**, open the **My puzzles** tab.
2. Optionally filter the list by household access.
3. Tick the puzzles you want to change, or use **Select all**.
4. Click **Set to Can manage**, **Set to View only** or
   **Use household default**.
5. Confirm with **Change access**.

You can change up to 200 puzzles at once. This list shows only your own
puzzles.

## Set the household default

The household default applies to every member's puzzles that have no access
setting of their own. A setting made for a specific puzzle always wins.

1. On **Household**, open the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Default access for puzzles**, choose **Can manage** or **View only**.
3. Confirm with **Change default**.

The page shows how many puzzles use the default. Only the household admin can
change it; other members see it read-only. A household starts with
**Can manage** as the default.

## Leave a household or remove a member

To leave, open the **Settings** tab, click **Leave household** and confirm. Your puzzles leave the
household with you, and you no longer see the others' puzzles.

The admin can remove a member: on the **Members** tab, click **⋯** next to
them, choose **Remove from household** and confirm. Their puzzles leave the household with them.

If the admin leaves, the longest-standing member becomes the admin. If you're
the last member, the household is dissolved and any pending invites are
cancelled.

A member who deletes their account leaves the household the same way, so if
they were the admin, the longest-standing member takes over.
