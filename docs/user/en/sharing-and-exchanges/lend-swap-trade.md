# Lend, Swap & Sell

JigSwap supports three kinds of exchanges:

- **Swap** — trade one of your puzzles for one of theirs (puzzle-for-puzzle).
- **Sale** — sell a puzzle for a price.
- **Lend (loan)** — let someone borrow a puzzle and get it back later.
  Ownership never changes hands on a loan.

> **Your history is safe.** When a puzzle changes ownership through a swap or
> sale, your completion history stays with _you_ — it doesn't transfer to the
> new owner.

**Where:** **Exchanges** (`/trades`).

## Offer your puzzle to swap, lend or sell

Other members can only request a puzzle you offer.

- **To swap it, or lend it to a friend:** turn on **Offer for swap** or
  **Offer to lend**, in the ⋯ menu on the puzzle's card in **My Puzzles** or
  with the switches on the puzzle's own page.
- **To sell it:** open the puzzle's page, click **Edit**, turn on **For Sale**
  under **Availability** and click **Save**. The puzzle's page then shows an
  **Offer for sale** switch: turn it off to stop selling.

When someone asks for your puzzle, you get a notification and the request
shows on **Exchanges** under **Incoming**. Click **Accept** (or **Decline**),
arrange the handover with **Message**, and once the puzzle has changed hands,
both of you click **Mark complete**. See
[The exchange lifecycle](#the-exchange-lifecycle), and for your puzzles' other
actions,
[Managing your puzzles](/docs/your-library/managing-your-puzzles#actions-on-each-puzzle).

## Find a copy

- **From a catalogue puzzle:** on the puzzle's page, click
  **Find a Copy to Swap** or **See all _N_ available copies →**. This opens
  **Browse** filtered to that puzzle. Remove the puzzle filter to see all
  puzzles again.
- **From Browse:** open any copy to see its page.

A copy's page shows its **Owner**. The name links to their profile when you
can see it. If the owner's profile is hidden from you, it shows
**Anonymous user**.

## Request a copy

1. Open the copy's page and click its request button. The button says what
   the owner offers: **Ask to borrow**, **Request swap** or **Ask to buy**, or
   **Request…** when they offer more than one. You can also use the request
   button on a copy's card in **Browse**.
2. In the **Request this puzzle** dialog, choose an **Exchange type**. Only the
   types the owner offers are listed:
   - **Swap** — choose the **Puzzle you offer** from your library. If you're in
     a household, puzzles you can manage there are listed too.
   - **Buy** — enter **Your offer (EUR)**. The owner sees your offer on the
     request before accepting or declining it.
   - **Borrow** — optionally choose when you'll bring it back under
     **Return by**: **No fixed date** (the default), **In 2 weeks**, **In 3
     weeks**, **In 1 month**, or **Pick a date…** from tomorrow on. The owner
     sees the date on the request before accepting or declining it.
3. Click **Send request**.

The owner receives the request, and you land on **Exchanges**. The dialog
tells you which of your details the owner will see. See
[Your identity in a trade](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/visibility-and-privacy#your-identity-in-a-trade).

> **Note:** You can't request your own puzzle, or send a second open request
> for the same puzzle. You can't request puzzles from a member who blocked you,
> or whom you blocked. A puzzle you only see through a circle where you have
> **View only** can't be requested either; see
> [What each permission allows](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/friend-circles#what-each-permission-allows).

## Message the owner about a copy

1. Open the copy's page and click **Message**.
2. Write your first message and send it.

The message shows the puzzle it is about. You and the owner have one
conversation, so if you've talked before, the message goes into your existing
conversation.

You can message an owner only if you're connected with them. If the owner keeps
their profile private, the copy page says you can't message them from there.
You can still request the copy.

## Manage your exchanges

The **Exchanges** page organizes everything into tabs:

- **All**
- **Incoming** — requests sent to you.
- **Outgoing** — requests you sent.
- **Loans** — every open loan you're part of, in two sections:
  - **Borrowed by you** — puzzles you're currently holding. Start or finish a
    solve, or log one, right from the row or from the puzzle's own page. When
    you have none, it links to **Browse Puzzles**.
  - **Lent out** — your puzzles (and household puzzles you may manage) that
    are out on loan, and who has them. In **My Puzzles**, the **Lent Out**
    filter shows the same puzzles among the rest of your collection.

A loan with a return date shows when it's due back, such as **Due in 5 days**.
Once it's due, it shows **Due today** or, after that, how many days it's
overdue.

On the other tabs, use the status filter to show only exchanges with one
status, such as **Pending** or **Completed**. The tab and the filter are part
of the page's address, so you can bookmark or share a view, and it stays the
same when you come back to the page.

Exchanges that wait on you (a request to answer, or a handover to confirm) are
listed first, under **Needs you**.

Click or tap an exchange to expand its details: both puzzles, the trade-partner
card and the actions for the exchange's current step. Click it again to fold
the details away.

You can also **Message** your exchange partner from the exchange to coordinate
details. To report a problem with a trade, open the **Trade actions** menu and
choose **Report member**. See
[Blocking & Reporting](/docs/discovering-and-community/blocking-and-reporting).

When you open a notification about an exchange, the **Exchanges** page opens
with that exchange expanded and highlighted.

## The exchange lifecycle

An exchange moves through clear steps:

1. **Pending** — the request has been sent.
2. **Accepted / Declined** — the recipient responds. (As the recipient of an
   incoming request, use **Accept** or **Decline**. As the sender of an
   outgoing request, you can **Cancel**.) **Decline** and **Cancel** ask you to
   confirm first, because they can't be undone; the other member gets a
   notification either way.
3. **Completion** — once accepted, **both parties must confirm** completion.
   Use **Mark complete** once the puzzle has actually changed hands. A dialog
   first tells you what completing does: for a swap or sale the puzzle moves to
   the other member's library and that can't be undone; for a loan, the loan
   starts. After you confirm, the exchange shows **You confirmed … · Waiting
   for …** until your partner confirms too. If your partner confirmed first,
   the exchange shows **Your turn**, and your confirmation completes it. Until
   then, either of you can still **Cancel** the exchange, for example if plans
   change or the other member stops responding.
4. **Completed** — when both confirm, ownership (swap/sale) or possession
   (loan) transfers, and you can **Leave a review** of your partner. For a
   completed loan, the status shows where the puzzle is: **On loan** while the
   borrower has it, then **Returned** or **Recalled** with the date it came
   back.

If an exchange doesn't go as planned, see
[Exchanges and loans](/docs/help/faq-and-troubleshooting#exchanges-and-loans)
in the FAQ.

### One puzzle, one deal

Accepting a request sets the puzzle aside for that exchange. Until the exchange
is completed or cancelled, you can't accept another request for the same
puzzle, whether to swap, buy or borrow it. Other members can still send you
requests for it, and you can accept one once the puzzle is free again. If the
exchange stalls, either of you can **Cancel** it, which frees the puzzle.

**Accept** doesn't go through while a puzzle in the exchange is set aside for
another exchange. **Accept** and **Mark complete** don't go through while a
puzzle in the exchange is out on loan, or once a puzzle has changed hands or is
gone since the request was sent. A message tells you why. An exchange whose
puzzle changed hands can't be completed any more: decline it if it's still a
request, or cancel it if it was accepted.

## Give back a puzzle you borrowed

If you've borrowed a puzzle (it's under **Loans › Borrowed by you**), use the
**Return** action to give it back. You're asked to confirm first: only return
it once the lender has the puzzle back, because the loan then closes for good.

## Get a lent puzzle back

Lent a puzzle to a friend and want it back? On **Exchanges**, open **Loans**:
under **Lent out** you see who has each puzzle you lent, and for how long.

- To arrange getting it back, click **Message** next to it, if you're
  connected with the borrower.
- Once you have the puzzle back, click **Recall** there, or on the puzzle's
  card in **My Puzzles**. You're asked to confirm first. The loan then closes
  for good, and the borrower doesn't get a notification.
- Until you've both clicked **Mark complete**, the loan hasn't started, and
  either of you can still **Cancel** the exchange.
- If the borrower stops answering or won't give the puzzle back, find the loan
  under **Incoming** and expand it, open its **Trade actions** menu and choose
  **Report member**, for example with **Fake trade or no-show**.

A puzzle can be on loan to one member at a time. While it's out on loan, it
isn't available: nobody can request it, you can't offer it again or offer it in
a swap, and you can't delete it. No exchange that would move it can be accepted
or completed until it's back. Once it's returned or recalled, turn on
**Offer for swap** or **Offer to lend** on its page to offer it again.
