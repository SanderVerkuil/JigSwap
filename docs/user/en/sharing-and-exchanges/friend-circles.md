# Friend Circles

Friend circles are private groups for sharing puzzles with people you trust,
such as family and close friends. Nobody is added to a circle without saying
yes: members are invited and join only when they accept.

**Where:** **Circles** (`/circles`).

## Create a circle

1. Open **Circles** and click **Create Circle**.
2. Enter a **Name** of 1 to 100 characters. A counter shows how many you've
   used.
3. Click **Create**.

You are the circle's **Owner** and its first admin. Each circle on the page
shows your role (**Owner** or **Member**), the member count and avatars. Admins
also get a settings button that opens the manage dialog.

## The circle page

Click a circle on **Circles** to open its own page. It shows:

- the circle's name, your role and the members' avatars, with **Manage** for
  admins and **Leave circle** for members;
- **Group chat**: the latest messages, and **Open group chat** to join the
  conversation;
- **Shared puzzles**: the most recent puzzles shared in the circle. Click one
  to open it.

Only members can open a circle's page. For anyone else, it doesn't exist.

## Group chat

Every circle has a group chat for its members. Click **Open group chat** on
the circle, on **Circles** or on its page.

- Only current members can read and write in it.
- New members see messages sent after they joined, not the earlier ones.
- If you leave or are removed, you lose access to the chat. The messages you
  sent stay in it for the others.
- If you blocked someone in the circle, their messages are collapsed for you.
  They still see yours, and they aren't told.

For muting, typing, online status and notifications, see
[Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages#circle-group-chats).

## Invite members

Any circle **Admin** can invite. You can invite people you follow mutually
(you follow each other).

1. On **Circles**, click the settings button on the circle.
2. Under **Invite**, search for the person.
3. Choose their **Permission**: **View only**, **Can exchange** or **Admin**.
4. Click **Send invite**.

The person gets a notification. Their invite shows under **Pending invites**
with when you sent it and when it expires. An invite expires after 14 days.

To cancel an invite, click **Revoke** and confirm. You can invite them again
later.

> **Note:** A circle can have at most 50 members, and pending invites count
> toward that limit.

## Accept or decline an invitation

1. Open the invitation notification, or go to **Circles**. Your invites are
   listed under **Invitations**, with who invited you, the member count and
   when the invite expires.
2. Click **Accept** to join, or **Decline**.

If an invite has expired or was revoked, you see "This invitation is no longer
available."

## Manage members and permissions

In the manage dialog, every Admin can:

- change a member's permission level: **View only**, **Can exchange** or **Admin**;
- remove a member;
- invite members and revoke pending invites;
- share copies into the circle.

The owner's seat can't be changed or removed.

## Share a copy with a circle

1. Open the circle's manage dialog.
2. Under **Share a copy**, choose one of your copies.
3. Click **Share**.

Circle members can see a shared copy while you offer it for swap, lend or
sale and you're still a member of the circle.

Shares end automatically:

- **When you leave or are removed**, the copies you shared into that circle
  are no longer shared there.
- **When a copy changes owner**, for example after a swap or sale, it is no
  longer shared in any circle. The new owner can share it again.

## Who you see in a circle

Members of the same circle see each other's names and avatars, even when a
profile is private. Bios, locations and private profiles stay hidden.

Being in the same circle doesn't show whether someone is online: only people
who follow each other and both share their online status see that. Everyone in
the circle's chat does see when someone is typing there.

## Leave a circle

1. On **Circles**, click **Leave circle** on the circle.
2. Confirm.

You lose access to the circle, its group chat and the puzzles shared in it.
Your messages stay in the chat. An admin can invite you again; you then see
only messages sent after you rejoined. The owner can't leave a circle; they can delete it instead.

If the owner deletes their account, the circle passes to the admin who has been
in it longest, or, without an admin, to the member who has been in it longest.
The new owner becomes an admin. If nobody else is left in the circle, it is
deleted. The former owner's seat shows as **Deleted member**; the new owner can
remove it.

## Delete a circle

Only the owner can delete a circle.

1. Open the circle's manage dialog and click **Delete circle**.
2. Type the circle's name exactly. The button stays disabled until the name
   matches.
3. Click **Delete circle**.

This removes the circle for everyone: members, invitations, the group chat and
its messages, and the puzzles shared in it. Shared puzzles stay in their owners' libraries. Members get a
notification. This can't be undone.
