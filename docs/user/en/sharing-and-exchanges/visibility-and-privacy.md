# Visibility & Privacy

You decide who sees each puzzle and how much of your profile other members
see. This page explains who sees what, and how to change it.

**Where:** a copy's page (**Edit** → **Who can see this copy**) and your
profile (`/profile` → **Edit Profile** → **Private profile**).

## Who sees a copy

Who can see a copy depends on whether you offer it, who the other member is,
and the copy's own setting:

- **Copies you offer** for swap, lend or sale can be found by members who can
  reach them: all members, if your profile is public, and members of friend
  circles you've shared the copy into.
- **Mutual followers** (you follow each other) see your whole library on your
  profile, including puzzles you don't offer.
- **Your household** sees all your puzzles in **My Puzzles**. See
  [Households](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/households).
- **Someone who is holding the copy**, such as a borrower, can always open it.

On your profile, mutual followers and household members get an
**All puzzles** / **Available** filter. Everyone else sees only the puzzles you
offer, with the note "Puzzles _name_ offers to swap, lend or sell."

> **Note:** If you block a member, they can no longer see your copies. See
> [Blocking & Reporting](/docs/discovering-and-community/blocking-and-reporting).

## Make a copy private

A private copy is hidden from everyone except you and your household, even
from mutual followers.

1. Open the copy's page and click **Edit**.
2. Under **Who can see this copy**, choose
   **Private — only you and your household**.
3. Click **Save**.

The copy now shows a **Private** badge. To show it again, choose
**Visible to the community**. New copies are visible.

A private copy is still visible to:

- you and the members of your household;
- whoever is currently holding it;
- the other member of a trade the copy is part of, while the trade is pending
  or accepted, and after it completes. If the trade is declined or cancelled,
  the copy is private to them again.

> **Note:** Only a visible copy can be offered. While a copy is offered for
> swap, lend or sale, the private option is disabled. Withdraw the offer first.
> Turning on an offer makes a private copy visible again.

## Set your profile to public or private

1. Open **Profile** and click **Edit Profile**.
2. Turn **Private profile** on or off.

When your profile is private:

- Members you're not connected with see you as **Anonymous user**.
- Your profile page is locked for them: they see "This shelf is private" and
  none of your puzzles, completions or story.
- You and your mutual followers still see everything.

Members who share a friend circle with you also see your name and avatar, even
when your profile is private. Your bio and location stay hidden from them, and
your profile stays locked.

## Your identity in a trade

Trades show the other member's details only inside that one trade.

- **When you request a puzzle**, the owner sees your name, handle, email
  address and the reviews you've received, for that trade only. The request
  dialog tells you this before you send. If your profile is private, it adds
  "Your profile itself stays private."
- **The owner stays hidden from you** until they accept your request or reply
  in the trade's conversation. Until then, the trade shows "Their details
  appear once they accept or reply."
- If the owner declines without replying, they stay hidden.

On **Exchanges** (`/trades`), open a trade with **View** to see the
**trade-partner card**: name, handle, email, rating and the reviews they've
received. A link to their profile appears only if you could see that profile
anyway.

The details you see in one trade don't unlock the other member's profile,
library or activity. A second trade with the same member starts hidden again.

## Photos are checked before others see them

New photos of your puzzles and completions are checked automatically before
other members can see them.

- **You** see your new photo straight away, marked **Pending review**.
- **Other members** see a blurred placeholder marked **Pending review** until
  the check is done.
- If a photo is rejected, it disappears for everyone else.

Only photos that have passed the check can be used as a copy's cover for other
members.
