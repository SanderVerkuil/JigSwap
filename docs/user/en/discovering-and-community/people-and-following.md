# Social & Community Features

## People / Social Hub

**Where:** **People** (`/people`).

- See your **network**: everyone you follow and everyone who follows you (with
  a "Follows you" badge).
- **Follow / Unfollow** members, and **Message** members you're connected
  with (see [Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages)).
- Read an **activity feed** of recent community activity (completions,
  acquisitions, completed exchanges).
- Edit your **public profile** from here too.

## The activity feed

**Where:** **People → Activity** (`/people?tab=activity`), and the **Latest**
column on your [dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard).

Entries in the feed are clickable:

- Click a member's **name** to open their profile.
- Click the **rest of the entry** to open what it's about: the puzzle copy,
  the puzzles a member just added, or the exchange (only if you took part in
  it).

Entries only link to pages you're allowed to see. Puzzles added in bulk, and
past solves logged in bulk, don't appear in the feed.

## What following each other unlocks

When you and another member **follow each other**, you see their **whole
library** on their profile, not only the puzzles they offer to swap, lend or
sell. Use the **All puzzles** and **Available** filter to switch between the
two. Copies they've made private stay hidden.

Members who don't follow each other only see the puzzles on offer. For the
details, see
[Visibility & Privacy](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/visibility-and-privacy).

## Invite friends

### In person: scan a QR code

1. Open **Show my QR** (on **People** or your **Profile**).
2. Your friend scans the code with their phone.
3. If they already have an account, they tap **Follow each other** and you
   follow each other right away.

### With an invite link

1. Open **Show my QR** and use **Copy link** or **Share** to send your invite
   link. (The **Find your people** step of
   [Quick setup](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) offers the same link.)
2. Your friend signs up through the link.
3. They're asked whether you two should follow each other. Nobody follows
   anyone until they confirm.

> **Note:** You need a username before you can share your QR code or invite
> link. JigSwap asks you to set one if you haven't yet.

### By email

Invite a friend who isn't on JigSwap yet.

1. Go to **People → Find people**. Under **Not on JigSwap yet?**, enter their
   **Email address**. (The **Find your people** step of
   [Quick setup](/docs/getting-started/dashboard) offers the same form.)
2. Click **Invite by email**. You see **Invitation sent**.

Your friend gets one email, in your language, saying you invited them to
follow each other. It's valid for 14 days. No reminders follow, and JigSwap
keeps no copy of the address.

- **Sent invitations** shows when you sent each invitation and when it
  expires. To cancel one, click **Revoke** and confirm.
- You can send up to 10 email invitations a day. An address gets at most one
  invitation email every 30 days.

### When someone invited you

If you signed up through someone's invite link or email invitation, an
**Invitations** card on your dashboard and on **People** says who invited you,
for example "Sam invited you to follow each other":

- **Follow each other**: you and the inviter now follow each other. They're
  notified that you followed them.
- **Not now**: nothing changes, and the inviter isn't told.

Nobody follows anyone until you choose **Follow each other**. The invitation
expires after 30 days if you don't answer.

If you already have an account and someone invites your email address, you
don't get an email. The invitation reaches you in JigSwap instead, as the same
**Invitations** card.

## Comments & reviews

- **Puzzle reviews:** On a catalogue definition page, post a community comment
  with a 1–5 star rating.
- **Copy comments:** Discuss a specific copy on its detail page.
- **Photo comments:** In the photo lightbox, leave text comments on individual
  photos.
