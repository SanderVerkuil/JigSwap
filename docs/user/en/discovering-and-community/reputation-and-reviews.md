# Reputation & Reviews

After a completed exchange, either party can leave a **partner review** to help the community gauge reliability.

JigSwap has three kinds of review. This page is about partner reviews of the
member you exchanged with, which you leave on **Exchanges**. You review a
puzzle itself on its page in the
[catalogue](/docs/discovering-and-community/discovering-puzzles#the-puzzle-catalogue),
and after you [log a solve](/docs/your-library/completions-and-goals#log-a-solve)
you can give stars to the puzzle and to the copy you solved.

A partner review includes:

- An overall **1–5 star** rating.
- Four sub-scores: **communication**, **packaging**, **condition**, and **timeliness**.
- An optional **text comment**.

To leave one, complete an exchange (see
[Lend, swap & sell](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/lend-swap-trade#the-exchange-lifecycle))
and use **Leave a review** on the completed exchange (in **Exchanges**). You can't review yourself. Your received reviews roll up into a **reputation badge** (average rating + a credibility score) shown on your profile.
