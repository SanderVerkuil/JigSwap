# Discovering Puzzles — Browse & Catalogue

## Browse community puzzles

**Where:** **Browse** (`/browse`).

Browse searches all community-owned copies that are currently **available** (for trade, lend, or sale).

1. Open **Browse**.
2. Build your filter with the **query builder**, combining:
   - Free-text search.
   - Category, difficulty, condition.
   - Piece-count range.
   - Availability flags (for trade / lend / sale).
3. Results appear as a grid or list of cards, each showing the owner and a swap/lend/sale badge. The card's button names the offer: **Ask to borrow**, **Request swap**, **Ask to buy**, or **Request…** when the owner offers more than one. The icons message the owner and add a favourite, and clicking the card opens the copy. See [Request a copy](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/lend-swap-trade#request-a-copy).

## The puzzle catalogue

**Where:** **Puzzles catalogue** (`/puzzles`).

The catalogue is the community-curated list of approved puzzle definitions (not individual copies).

- Browse the infinite-scroll grid with filters.
- Open a definition (`/puzzles/<id>`) to see the cover, rating with breakdown, difficulty, category/tag chips, community stats (owners, completions, average solve days, copies available to swap), and a list of available copies with their owners. On a phone or tablet, pinch the cover to zoom in on it and drag to look around; double-tap to zoom back out.
- If you own it, you'll see an ownership banner linking to your copy.
- You can **add it to your library** from this page, **write a review**, or **contribute a new definition**.

## Fix wrong details on a catalogue puzzle

Spotted a wrong piece count, barcode or photo? Suggest a correction:

1. Open the puzzle in the **Puzzles catalogue** and click **Suggest an edit**.
2. Change what's wrong. Under **Why this change?** you can say how you know,
   for example that the box says 500 pieces.
3. Click **Submit suggestion**.

An admin checks your suggestion before the catalogue changes. You can have one
open suggestion per puzzle.

To follow your suggestions, open **My Puzzles**, click **⋯** (**More actions**)
and choose **My suggestions**. Each one shows whether it's **Pending**,
**Approved** or **Declined**, with the admin's reason. While it's pending, you
can **Edit** or **Withdraw** it. You also get a notification when it's approved
or declined; those notifications are under **My submissions** in your
[notification preferences](/docs/staying-informed/notifications#notification-preferences).
