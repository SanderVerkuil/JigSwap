# Blocking & Reporting

If someone bothers you, you can block them so they can't contact you. You can
also report a member, a message or a photo to our moderators.

**Where:** a member's profile, a conversation, a trade on **Exchanges**, and
your account's **Privacy & safety** page.

## Block a member

1. Open the member's profile and open the actions menu, or open your
   conversation with them and open the menu in the conversation header.
2. Choose **Block _name_**.
3. Read the confirmation and confirm.

A toast confirms the block and offers **Undo**.

What blocking does, as the confirmation explains:

> _Name_ won't be able to message you, follow you or propose trades. They
> won't be notified, but they'll see they can't reply. Accepted trades can
> still be completed. Shared circles or households aren't changed. If you
> unblock them later, follows removed by the block aren't restored.

Blocking also removes follows between you in both directions and ends trade
requests between you that were still waiting for an answer.

In your conversation with them, the message box is replaced by
"You blocked _name_." with an **Unblock** button.

## Unblock a member

You can unblock from the conversation, from their profile, or from
**Privacy & safety**. After you unblock, they can message you again if you're
connected. Follows removed by the block aren't restored.

## Report something

Reports are private: only our moderators see them. You can report from these
places:

- **A message:** open the **More** menu next to someone else's message (or
  press and hold it on a phone) and choose **Report message**.
- **A photo:** in the photo viewer, choose **Report photo**.
- **A member in a trade:** on **Exchanges**, open the trade, open the
  **Trade actions** menu and choose **Report member**.
- **A member:** choose **Report _name_** in the actions menu on their profile
  or in the conversation header.

## Fill in the report

1. Choose why you're reporting:
   - **Spam or scam**
   - **Harassment or hate**
   - **Sexual or inappropriate content**
   - **Fake trade or no-show**
   - **Illegal content**
   - **Something else**
2. Optionally, add **Details (optional)** (up to 1000 characters).
3. If you also want to block the member, tick **Also block _name_**. It is
   ticked for you when you choose **Harassment or hate** or
   **Sexual or inappropriate content**.
4. For **Illegal content**, you must also tick "I confirm in good faith that
   this information is accurate and complete." Your name and email from your
   account are included with this notice, as the law requires.
5. Click **Send report**.

The member you report won't know who reported them. We review the report and
send you a notification with our decision.

## Privacy & safety

**Where:** open your account menu → **Manage account** → **Privacy & safety**.

This page shows:

- **Blocked members** — everyone you've blocked. Click **Unblock** next to a
  member and confirm to unblock them. Only you can see this list.
- **Your reports** — each report you've sent, with its status
  (**Under review** or **Handled**).
