# Your Profile & Your Shelf

**Where:** **Profile** (`/profile`).

Your profile is your public face on JigSwap. It shows:

- An **identity header**: avatar, name, a meta row, and your trust/reputation badge.
- An **edit toggle** that opens an inline form to update your **display name**, **bio**, **location**, and other identity fields.
  The same form sets your **Username** and **Custom link**. Your profile
  address uses one of them, and **Show my QR** needs it (see
  [Invite friends](/docs/discovering-and-community/people-and-following#invite-friends)).
- **"{Your Name}'s Shelf"**: your boxes standing on a plank, your newest copies or the ones you chose. Each box opens that puzzle.
- Four **stat columns**.
- A **received reputation** section showing reviews others have left you.
- A **Privacy** section where you choose who can see your profile (public or private) and can reset your invite link. Changes there are saved straight away.

You can also **arrange your shelf** to feature up to six copies.

## Play with your shelf

Press **Play** under a shelf to turn it 3D. The boxes stay where they stand,
and you can grab and throw them. **Reset** puts every box back. Play isn't
offered when your device asks for reduced motion, saves data, or has little
memory.

> The same shelf comes back across JigSwap: on your **dashboard**, on other
> members' profiles, for each **collection**, and on your **household**'s
> Puzzles tab.
