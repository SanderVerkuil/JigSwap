# Completions & Goals

## Recording completions

JigSwap keeps a full **solve log** of every puzzle you've worked on — even after you trade the puzzle away, your completion records stay with you.

**Where:** **Completions** (`/completions`).

### Log a completion

1. Go to **My Puzzles**.
2. On the copy you solved, click **Log a solve**.
3. In the dialog, choose **Finished** (the default) or **Still solving**. Both
   dates start on today. With **Still solving** you only set the start date,
   and you finish the solve later.
4. For a finished solve, set the **start and end dates** and, if you like,
   answer **How long did it take?** in hours and minutes. Add optional
   **notes**. After saving, you can give the puzzle a 1–5 star rating and a
   **review**.

Don't want to record solve times? Click **Don't track times** under the time
fields. To turn them back on, open your account menu → **Manage account** →
**Preferences**.

You can also start from **Completions**: open **Log a completion**
(`/completions/new`) and pick the puzzle. The list shows the solves you're still
working on first (pick one to finish it), then your own puzzles, the puzzles
you've borrowed, and your household's puzzles. Use **Search** to find one by
title, brand or owner.

> You can attach up to **5 photos** per completion, and edits are allowed within a **24-hour** window after completing.

### Log puzzles you finished in the past

If you've solved puzzles before joining JigSwap, you can log several at once,
even when you don't remember exactly when.

**Where:** **Quick setup → Log puzzles you finished** (`/welcome?step=solves`).
Open Quick setup from your account menu.

1. The step lists the puzzles in your library that have no solve logged yet.
   Click **Show more** to see more.
2. Tick **I finished** next to each puzzle you've solved.
3. Under **When?**, choose one:
   - **I don't remember** (the default),
   - **Which year**, then pick the year, or
   - **Pick a date**.
4. Click **Log N puzzles** (for example, **Log 3 puzzles**).

You can log up to 25 puzzles at a time. These solves don't appear in the
community activity feed.

How the date you choose is used:

- A solve with only a year shows as "In 2023"; one without a date shows as
  "Date unknown".
- Solves without a date don't count toward your goals. A solve with only a
  year counts toward a goal whose period includes part of that year.
- Neither has a solve time, and neither is included in your monthly solve
  trends.
- To add an exact date later, **Edit** the solve on the Completions page
  within the 24-hour edit window.

### The Completions page

The Completions page shows all your solve records, newest first, with a three-stat summary at the top: **total finished**, **pieces placed**, and **finishes this year**.

Each row shows the title, a status badge, a piece/days line, optional notes and review quote, a star rating, and the finish date. Per-row actions:

- **Finish** — for an in-progress solve.
- **Add/Edit Review** — set or change your star rating and review text.
- **Edit** — adjust date range, time, and notes. Once a solve's edit window
  has closed, the button is greyed out: hover over it or tap it to see when it
  closed.

## Goals

Set personal challenges and track them automatically against your solve log.

**Where:** **Goals** (`/goals`).

Until you have a goal, the page explains what a goal is and offers
**New Goal**.

### Create a goal

1. Open **Goals** and click **New Goal**.
2. Fill in:
   - A **title** (required) and an optional **description**.
   - A **target completions** count (required): a whole number of 1 or more.
   - A **period**: **From** and **To**. A new goal covers this calendar year
     (1 January to 31 December) unless you change it. Leave **To** empty for
     a goal without an end date.
3. Click **Create goal**. If something is missing, the form says what to fix
   next to the field.

### How progress counts

A goal counts the solves you finish inside its period, from the first day to
the last day:

- A solve with a finish date counts when that date falls in the period.
- A solve with only a year counts when that year overlaps the period.
- A solve without a date never counts.

Solves you logged before you created the goal count too, as long as they fall
in the period. Progress updates automatically when you log, edit or delete a
solve; you never update a goal by hand.

### Your goals page

Goals are grouped into **Active**, **Achieved** and **Past goals**. Each goal
shows its period, a **progress bar**, a percentage and your current/target
count. An achieved goal gets a **trophy badge**. **Past goals** (collapsed
until you open it) holds goals whose period ended before you reached the
target (**Ended**) and goals you archived (**Archived**).

### Edit, archive or delete a goal

Open the **⋯** menu on a goal:

- **Edit** — change the title, description, target or period. Progress is
  recounted for the new period.
- **Archive** — move the goal to **Past goals**. It stops counting new solves
  until you choose **Restore**, which recounts it.
- **Delete** — remove the goal for good, after you confirm. Your solves are not
  affected.
