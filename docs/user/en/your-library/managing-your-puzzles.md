# Managing Your Puzzle Library

Your library is the set of physical puzzle **copies** you personally own. (A "copy" is one physical puzzle you own — the platform tracks each copy separately so your history stays with you.)

**Where:** **My Puzzles** (`/my-puzzles`).

## View your puzzles

1. Open **My Puzzles** from the sidebar. On a phone, tap the **Library** tab,
   then **My Puzzles**.
2. Switch between **grid** and **list** layouts.
3. Type in the **search box** to find puzzles by title, brand or tag. Every
   word you type has to match; capitals and accents don't matter, and you can
   type a tag with or without its **#**.
4. Pick an order in the **sort** menu: **Recently added** (the default),
   **Title A–Z** or **Fewest pieces first**.
5. Use the **status filters** to narrow what you see:
   - **All**
   - **Available**
   - **For Trade**
   - **For Loan**
   - **Lent Out**
   - **In Progress**
   - **Completed**
6. In a household with someone else in it, the **Whose** menu shows everyone's
   puzzles, only yours, or only your housemates'.

On a phone, the status filters, **Whose** and the sort order are behind the
**Filters** button next to the search box. Filters that are on show as chips
under it: tap one to remove it.

Your search, sort, filters and layout are kept in the page address, so they're
still there when you come back from a puzzle, reload the page or share the
link. If nothing matches, the page says which search or filter left it empty;
**Show all** clears them.

Each puzzle card shows its condition (a coloured dot and its word, such as **Good**), what it's offered for (green chips with an icon for swap, lend and sale), its tags as _#tag_, and its loan status (who currently has it, if lent out). It also gives you quick actions.

## Actions on each puzzle

Click a puzzle card to open its detail page. The card's ⋯ menu offers:

- **Log a solve** or **Start puzzle** — record that you've completed (or started) the puzzle.
- **Edit** — change the copy's details.
- **Offer for swap** and **Offer to lend** — switch that offer on or off, with an Undo.
- **Share with circles…** — share the copy with circles you pick.
- **Add to collection** — put the copy in one of your collections.
- **Delete** — remove the copy from your library (with confirmation).

A lent-out puzzle's card also has a **Recall** button: once the puzzle is back
with you, use it to close the loan. JigSwap asks you to confirm first, then
tells you whether it worked.

## Change many puzzles at once

Click **Select** next to the puzzle count, then click the puzzles you want, or
**Select all** for everything the current search and filters show. A bar at the
bottom of the list then offers:

- **Offer for swap** and **Offer to lend**: adds that offer and keeps any other
  offers a puzzle already has. Unless your screen is very wide, these and
  **Withdraw offers** sit under **Offers**.
- **Withdraw offers**: takes every offer off the selected puzzles.
- **Add to collection**: puts the selected puzzles in a collection you pick.
- **Share with circle**: shares the selected puzzles with a circle you pick.
  Circle members see a shared puzzle while it's on offer.

A message then says what changed and what didn't, with **Undo** for offers. A
puzzle that's out on loan or in an ongoing exchange can't be offered, but you
can always withdraw an offer. These actions never change your choice of who can see a copy: a
private copy is visible while it's on offer and private again once you withdraw
every offer.

You can select a housemate's puzzle only if they let you manage it. It stays out
of your own collections, and only its owner can share it with a circle. **Done**
ends the selection.

## The copy detail page

**Where:** click a puzzle card, or go to `/my-puzzles/<id>`.

The detail page is the rich record of a single owned copy. It includes:

- A **hero image** with condition and difficulty chips. On a phone, tap it to
  see it large. There, and on a tablet, pinch it to zoom in and drag to look
  around; double-tap to zoom back out.
- A **status line** under the title. On your own copy it says where the copy
  is and who can see it: "On loan to _name_ since _date_" (with **Recall**);
  what it's offered for and who can see it now: the community if your profile
  is public, friends and shared circles if it's private, or only while offered
  if you chose **Private**; or, when it isn't offered, that friends can see it
  or that it's private. Other members only see what it's offered for.
- **Acquisition details** (when and how you got it) and your private notes.
  Only you (and household members who can manage the copy) see them. If
  nothing is recorded yet, click **Add details** to fill in the month, year
  and source.
- A **stats strip**: times completed, fastest finish (from the times solvers entered), times lent out, and your
  own rating of the copy (shown to you and your household, or to anyone who
  rated it).
- The **Owner**: their avatar and name, which links to their profile when you're
  allowed to see it. On your own copy it reads your name followed by "· you".
  If the owner keeps their profile hidden from you, it shows
  **Anonymous user**.
- A **photo strip** with a lightbox. As the owner you can upload photos, set a cover image, and leave per-photo comments.
- A two-column **history**: your completion timeline (with ratings), lending history (with status labels), and swap/transfer history.
  If someone in that history has deleted their account, their entries stay
  and show **Deleted member**, without a name, picture or link.
- A **community rating breakdown** and a comment area.

**Owner actions** on this page include the **Offer for swap** and **Offer to
lend** switches (and **Offer for sale**, shown only while the copy is for
sale), **Log a solve**, and editing. Each switch change is
confirmed with an **Undo**, and it never changes who can see the copy (see
below). While the copy is out on loan you can't switch an offer on until it's
back. Use **Recall** once you have the puzzle back: it asks you to confirm
first, then ends the loan and marks the puzzle as back with you.
**Visitors** (other members) see a public version instead, with a request
button named after the offer, such as **Request swap** or **Ask to borrow**,
and **Message**.

**Copy reviews** are star ratings of this physical copy. Anyone who logs a
solve of the copy is asked to rate it.

## Photos

### View photos full size

Click a photo in the photo strip to open the lightbox. In it you can:

- Use the arrows, the arrow keys, or swipe to move between photos.
- Zoom in by pinching, by double-tapping (double-tap again to zoom back out),
  or with the **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Reset zoom** buttons.
- Drag to move around a zoomed-in photo.

### Crop or rotate a photo

When you add a photo, an editor opens where you can crop and rotate it before
it's uploaded. Some browsers block photo editing to protect your privacy (for
example Firefox with fingerprinting protection turned on). There, a photo you
didn't change is uploaded as it is. If you did change it, JigSwap tells you the
changes can't be applied and offers to upload the original photo instead. The
**Auto crop** button isn't shown in those browsers, because it can't read the
photo there.

### Photos waiting for review

Every photo you upload is checked automatically before other members can see
it. Until then:

- You see your photo as normal, marked **Pending review**.
- Other members see a blurred placeholder with **Pending review** instead.

Only approved photos can be used as a cover.

## Who can see a copy

Each copy is either visible or private. Set it when you **Edit** the copy,
under **Who can see this copy**:

- **Visible**: members you follow each other with can see it in your library.
  While it's on offer, so can all members if your profile is public, and
  members of circles you've shared it into if it's private.
- **Private — only you and your household**: hidden from everyone else,
  friends included.

A copy you offer for swap, lend or sale is visible while the offer lasts. If
you chose **Private**, it stays your choice: once you withdraw every offer the
copy is private again. For the full picture, see
[Visibility & Privacy](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/visibility-and-privacy).

## Household copies

If you're in a household, your housemates' copies appear in your library too,
marked with a **Household** badge and the owner's name. What you can do with
them depends on the access they gave you; see
[Households](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/households).
