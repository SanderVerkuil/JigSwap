# Managing Your Puzzle Library

Your library is the set of physical puzzle **copies** you personally own. (A "copy" is one physical puzzle you own — the platform tracks each copy separately so your history stays with you.)

**Where:** **My Puzzles** (`/my-puzzles`).

## View your puzzles

1. Open **My Puzzles** from the sidebar or tab bar.
2. Switch between **grid** and **list** layouts.
3. Use the **status filter pills** to narrow what you see:
   - **All**
   - **Available**
   - **For Trade**
   - **For Lend**
   - **In Progress**
   - **Completed**

Each puzzle card shows its loan status (who currently has it, if lent out) and gives you quick actions.

## Actions on each puzzle

From a puzzle card you can:

- **View** — open the full copy detail page.
- **Edit** — change the copy's details.
- **Delete** — remove the copy from your library (with confirmation).
- **Log Solve** — record that you've completed (or started) the puzzle.
- **Recall** — if a puzzle is currently lent out, recall it to get it back.

## The copy detail page

**Where:** click **View** on a puzzle, or go to `/my-puzzles/<id>`.

The detail page is the rich record of a single owned copy. It includes:

- A **hero image** with the availability badge and condition/difficulty chips.
- **Acquisition details** (how/when you got it) and your private notes.
- A **stats strip**: times completed, fastest finish, times lent out, and average rating.
- The **Owner**: their avatar and name, which links to their profile when you're
  allowed to see it. On your own copy it reads your name followed by "· you".
  If the owner keeps their profile hidden from you, it shows
  **Anonymous user**.
- A **photo strip** with a lightbox. As the owner you can upload photos, set a cover image, and leave per-photo comments.
- A two-column **history**: your completion timeline (with ratings), lending history (with status pills), and swap/transfer history.
- A **community rating breakdown** and a comment area.

**Owner actions** on this page include toggling _for-trade_ / _for-lend_, logging a completion, and editing.
**Visitors** (other members) see a public version with _Request Swap_ and _Message_ actions instead.

## Photos

### View photos full size

Click a photo in the photo strip to open the lightbox. In it you can:

- Use the arrows, the arrow keys, or swipe to move between photos.
- Zoom in by pinching, by double-tapping (double-tap again to zoom back out),
  or with the **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Reset zoom** buttons.
- Drag to move around a zoomed-in photo.

### Photos waiting for review

Every photo you upload is checked automatically before other members can see
it. Until then:

- You see your photo as normal, marked **Pending review**.
- Other members see a blurred placeholder with **Pending review** instead.

Only approved photos can be used as a cover.

## Who can see a copy

Each copy is either visible or private. Set it when you **Edit** the copy,
under **Who can see this copy**:

- **Visible to the community**: members you follow each other with can see it
  in your library.
- **Private — only you and your household**: hidden from everyone else,
  friends included.

A copy you offer for swap, lend or sale is always visible. For the full
picture, see
[Visibility & Privacy](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/visibility-and-privacy).

## Household copies

If you're in a household, your housemates' copies appear in your library too,
marked with a **Household** badge and the owner's name. What you can do with
them depends on the access they gave you; see
[Households](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/households).
