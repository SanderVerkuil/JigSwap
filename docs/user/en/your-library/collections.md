# Collections — Organizing Your Shelves

Collections are named shelves you create to organize your copies.

**Where:** **Collections** (`/collections`).

Until you have a collection, the page explains what one is and offers
**New Collection**.

## Create a collection

1. Open **Collections**.
2. Click **New Collection**.
3. Set:
   - A **name**. **Create** stays off until the collection has one. If you
     already have a collection with that name, the dialog says so under the
     field.
   - An **emoji icon**.
   - A **color**.
   - A **description**.
   - **Visibility** — public or private.
4. Click **Create**. The new collection opens straight on its **Add Puzzles**
   page, so you can fill it right away.

## Add puzzles to a collection

1. Open a collection tile to see its detail.
2. Use **Add puzzles** (`/collections/<id>/add-puzzles`) to attach copies from
   your library. Every puzzle in your library can go into a collection,
   including ones you keep for yourself, private ones and ones that are lent
   out; puzzles already in this collection aren't listed again.
3. Select puzzles and press **Add**. If a puzzle couldn't be added, a message
   says how many, and those stay selected so you can try again.

When the list is empty, the page says why: your library is empty (with a link
to add a puzzle), everything is already in this collection, or nothing
matches your search.

## Take a puzzle out of a collection

On the collection's page, open the **⋯** menu on a puzzle card and choose
**Remove from this collection**. The puzzle stays in your library. The
confirmation has an **Undo** button that puts it back.

## Open a puzzle from a collection

Clicking one of your own puzzles in a collection opens it on its **My
Puzzles** page, with the collection in the breadcrumb so you can step back to
it.

## Edit or delete a collection

- On **Collections**, each tile opens its collection; the gear button on the
  tile (**Edit**) opens the editor. On a collection's page, use **Edit**.
- **Delete collection** in the editor deletes the collection after you
  confirm. The puzzles in it stay in your library. Your default collection
  can't be deleted.

Only you see **Add Puzzles**, **Edit** and the card **⋯** menu on your
collections. Others who open a public collection see its puzzles without them.

Each collection tile shows its puzzle count and whether it's the default.
