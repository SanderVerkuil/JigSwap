# Insights & Stats

**Where:** **Insights** (`/insights`).

Your personal analytics dashboard shows:

- A headline row with your **completions**, **total solve time**, **puzzles
  owned** and **exchanges completed**, and a second row with your
  **collections**, **distinct titles**, **goals achieved** (with, for example,
  "2 of 5 goals" below it once you have a goal; archived goals count too) and
  the **average ratings** you gave and received.
- **Completions / month**: a bar per month over the past 12 months, with the
  number above each bar. Switch between **Completions** and **Solve minutes**
  with the pills next to the title.
- **Swaps / month**: the swaps you started in each of the past 12 months,
  quiet months included.
- A **collection breakdown**: your puzzles by piece count, by difficulty (a bar with a legend that lists each difficulty with its count and share) and your top brands. Puzzles without a brand are counted under the brand chart instead of ranking as a brand.
- A **data export** button to download your personal data.

**Before your first finished solve**, the page starts with **No finished solves
yet** and two buttons: **Log a solve** and **Set a goal**. The solve figures and
the Completions / month chart appear as soon as you finish a solve; everything
else is shown from the start.

## Export your data

**Export my data** downloads a JSON copy of your puzzles, completions,
collections, goals and exchanges, named `jigswap-export-<date>.json`. You find
it in three places:

- at the bottom of **Insights**;
- on your account's **Privacy & safety** page (open your account menu →
  **Manage account** → **Privacy & safety**), under **Your data**;
- in quick search (**Cmd/Ctrl + K**), under **Quick Actions**: choosing it
  starts the download straight away.

Very long lists are cut off at a fixed number of rows per category; the
confirmation tells you when that happened.
