# Finding Your Way Around

Once signed in, JigSwap presents a **dashboard** with a few consistent navigation pieces:

- **On desktop:** a top bar (with a search pill) and a grouped sidebar on the left holding links to all your areas (Library, Community, etc.). Your content appears in a card in the middle.
- **On mobile (phones/small screens):** a slim top bar plus a **bottom tab bar**. A center **quick-action button** opens a sheet with common actions.

Most pages also show a **primary action button** (for example, _Add Puzzle_) and any relevant counts in the page header.

## Your account menu

Click your avatar to open the account menu. Besides **Profile**,
**Preferences**, **Notifications** and **Privacy & safety**, it has:

- **What's new**: recent features and fixes (see below).
- **Quick setup**: reopens the guided setup for your shelf. See
  [Your Dashboard](/docs/getting-started/dashboard#quick-setup).

## What's new

**Where:** **What's new** in your account menu (`/whats-new`).

The What's new page lists recent features, improvements and fixes, newest
first. Updates you haven't seen yet are marked **New**, and the menu item shows
how many there are, for example **What's new (2)**.

When there's something new, JigSwap may also show it in a short dialog. Click
**Read more** to open the full entry, or **Maybe later** to close it.

The page has two tabs: **Updates** (the list above) and **Guides**.

### Guides

The **Guides** tab (`/whats-new?tab=guides`) lists every guided tour in one
place, grouped by area, with how many you've finished. Each guide shows how
many steps it has and where you are: **Step 2 of 3** when you stopped partway,
**Done** when you finished it. A guide that came with an update links back to
it with **Introduced** and the month.

Some updates also come with a short tour of the feature. Click
**Take the tour** on the update or on its guide, then use **Next** and
**Back** to move through it, or **End tour** to stop.

- Some steps ask you to try the feature. They show "Press the highlighted
  button to continue." Press the highlighted button and the tour moves on.
- If you leave a tour partway, you can resume it from its guide on the
  **Guides** tab, with **Resume tour** (or **Start over**). An update's tour
  also resumes from the update, and the tour from Quick setup from Quick
  setup.
- **Replay the tour** runs a finished tour again.
- On **Exchanges**, **Circles**, **Completions** and **Add Puzzle**, the
  **?** button in the page header (**Show me how**) starts that page's guide
  at its step on that page.

## Install JigSwap on your phone

You can add JigSwap to your phone's home screen and open it like an app: full
screen, one tap away. On iPhone and iPad, this is also what lets JigSwap send
you push notifications.

**On Android (Chrome and similar browsers):**

1. Tap the center **quick-action button** in the tab bar.
2. Tap **Install app**.
3. Confirm in your browser's prompt.

If Chrome hasn't offered the install prompt yet, the tip shows two steps
instead: tap the **menu** at the top right of Chrome, then choose **Add to
home screen** or **Install app**, and then **Install**.

**On Android (Firefox):** Firefox doesn't offer an install prompt, so the tip
shows the steps:

1. Tap the **⋮ menu** in Firefox's toolbar (at the top or bottom).
2. Choose **Add app to Home screen** or **Add to Home screen**, then confirm.

**On iPhone and iPad (Safari, or Chrome, Edge and Firefox on recent iOS versions):**

1. Tap **Share** (under **⋯** if you don't see it; in Firefox it's in the
   address bar or Firefox's menu).
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.

The **Install app** item in the quick-action sheet shows these steps too.

> **Tip:** From your second visit on a phone, JigSwap shows an
> **Add JigSwap to your home screen** tip above the tab bar (on Android Chrome
> it has its own **Install app** button). Tap **Not now** to
> hide it for 30 days, or close it to stop it appearing. You can still install
> from the quick-action sheet at any time. The tip doesn't appear once JigSwap
> is installed.
