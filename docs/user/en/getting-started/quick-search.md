# Quick Search (Command Palette)

JigSwap has a global search that finds anything quickly.

- **Open it:** press **Cmd/Ctrl + K**, or click the search box (**Search puzzles, people, circles…**) in the desktop top bar.
- **With nothing typed:** you'll see jump-to navigation destinations and quick-action shortcuts. **Export my data** is one of them: it downloads your data straight away.
- **Type 2+ characters:** live results appear, grouped into **In your library** (your own copies, which open on your My Puzzles page), **Puzzles** (the catalogue), **People**, **Circles**, and **Collections**. **In your library** looks through your 500 most recently added copies; to search all of them, use the search box on **My Puzzles**.
- **Select a result** to navigate straight there.
