# Your Dashboard (Morning Briefing)

**Where:** the home screen after sign-in (`/dashboard`).

Your dashboard is a personal briefing that summarizes your puzzle life at a glance:

- A **headline sentence** summarizing your active exchanges and goal progress.
- A **pending-request banner** if someone has sent you an incoming exchange request that needs a response.
- **Quick-action chips**: _Add Puzzle_, _Browse_, and _Log Completion_.
- A **3D shelf** ("plank") showing your real puzzle copies as physical objects you can look at.
- **Live stats**: how many puzzles you own, trades completed, your average rating, and active exchanges.
- A **pulse section**: exchanges in motion, goal progress, and your latest activity.
- A **fresh-puzzles scroller** to discover newly added community puzzles.

## Quick setup

Quick setup walks you through filling your shelf in a few minutes. You can
stop at any time and pick it up later.

**Where:** **Quick setup** in your account menu, or the **Quick setup** card on
your dashboard (`/welcome`).

When you first join, a **Welcome to JigSwap!** dialog offers
**Start quick setup** or **Explore on my own**. Either way, Quick setup stays
available from your account menu.

The steps are:

1. **Add your puzzles**: pick the puzzles you own from the catalogue, several
   at once. See [Adding & Importing Puzzles](/docs/your-library/adding-and-importing).
2. **Log puzzles you finished**: mark the ones you've already solved, with a
   date, a year, or "I don't remember". See
   [Completions & Goals](/docs/your-library/completions-and-goals).
3. **Find your people**: follow people you may know, search for members, or
   share your invite link. See
   [People & Following](/docs/discovering-and-community/people-and-following).
4. **Household** (optional): create a household, accept an invitation, or
   choose **I puzzle alone**.
5. **Make it yours** (optional): set your photo, location, and who can see your
   profile.

Use **Continue** to go to the next step, **Skip** to pass a step, or
**Finish later** to leave. When you're done, **Go to my shelf** returns you to
your dashboard, and **Show me around** starts a short guided tour.

### The Quick setup card

New members also see a **Quick setup** card on the dashboard. It shows how
many steps are done and links to each one. To hide it, click
**Hide quick setup**; you can reopen it from your account menu.

## Invitations

If you joined through someone's invite link, an **Invitations** card asks
whether you two should follow each other. See
[People & Following](/docs/discovering-and-community/people-and-following#when-someone-invited-you).
