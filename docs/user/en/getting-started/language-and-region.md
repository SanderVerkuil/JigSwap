# Language & Theme

JigSwap is available in **English (en)** and **Dutch (nl)**, with a light or a
dark look.

## Change the language

- **In the app:** click the globe button at the top right
  (**Switch language**) and choose **English** or **Nederlands**.
- **On a phone:** tap **+** in the bottom bar; **Language** is at the bottom of
  the sheet.
- **In your account:** open your account menu → **Manage account** →
  **Preferences**, under **Language**.
- **On public pages:** use the flag in the header (on a phone, in the ☰ menu).

The interface (and the sign-in screens) switch immediately, and your choice is remembered for future visits.

JigSwap automatically picks a starting language based on your saved preference, then your browser's language, defaulting to English.

## Light or dark

- **In the app:** click the sun or moon button just left of the globe
  (**Toggle theme**) and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**.
- **On a phone:** tap **+** in the bottom bar; **Theme** is at the bottom of
  the sheet.
- **In your account:** open your account menu → **Manage account** →
  **Preferences**, under **Theme**.
- **On public pages:** the moon or sun button in the header switches between
  light and dark (on a phone, in the ☰ menu).

**System** follows your device's light or dark setting, and it's where new
members start. Your choice is kept in this browser.
