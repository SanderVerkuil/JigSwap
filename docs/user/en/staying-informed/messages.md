# Messages

**Where:** **Community → Messages** (`/messages`).

Messages is where you talk to other members one on one: about a puzzle you
spotted, a trade you're working out, or where to meet up. Each conversation is
with one member, and every exchange has its own conversation too.

## Who you can message

You can start a conversation with a member when at least one of these is true:

- You follow each other.
- You're both in the same friend circle.
- You already have a conversation or an exchange together.

If none of these apply, the **Message** button is disabled and explains
"You can only message members you're connected with". Blocking a member, in
either direction, also stops new messages between you.

## The inbox

On desktop, the inbox sits on the left and the open conversation on the right.
On a phone, you see the inbox first; opening a conversation fills the whole
screen, and the back button returns you to the inbox.

Each row in the inbox shows:

- The member's name and how long ago the last message was sent (for example
  _now_, _5m_, _2h_, a weekday, or a date).
- A one-line preview of the last message, starting with "You:" when you sent
  it.
- A count of unread messages, if there are any.
- The cover of the latest puzzle the conversation is about, when there is one.

## Start a conversation

**From a member's profile or their tile on People:**

1. Click **Message**.
2. JigSwap opens your conversation with that member, or starts a new one.

**About a specific puzzle:**

1. Open the puzzle copy (from **Browse** or a member's profile).
2. Click **Message**.
3. Write your first message. The puzzle is attached to it, so the owner knows
   which one you mean.
4. Send it. The conversation starts when you send the first message.

If you already have a conversation with the owner, JigSwap takes you straight
to it. You can't message about your own puzzle, and some owners keep their
profile private, in which case you can't message them from their puzzle.

## Read a conversation

- Messages are grouped by day, under **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the date.
- Messages one person sends within a few minutes of each other are grouped
  together, and the time shows once per group.
- When you scroll to the top, older messages load automatically. You can also
  press **Load earlier messages**. When you reach the beginning, you see
  **Start of the conversation**.

### Quick links in the header

The conversation header has shortcuts to what the conversation is about:

- **View profile** opens the other member's profile.
- **View exchange** opens the exchange, for a conversation about a trade, loan
  or sale.
- When the conversation covers two or more puzzles, a stack of covers opens a
  **Puzzles** menu listing each one and whose copy it is.

On a phone, **View profile** and **View exchange** are in the
**Conversation options** menu.

## Attach a puzzle

You can point the other member at any puzzle, yours or theirs:

1. In the message box, click **Attach** (the **+** button).
2. Choose **Attach a puzzle**.
3. Pick from the **My puzzles** tab, or from the other member's tab (for
   example **Mara's puzzles**). Use **Search puzzles** to filter by title or
   brand.
4. The puzzle appears above the message box. Write your message and send it.

You only see puzzles that both of you are allowed to see. A message always
needs some text, even with a puzzle attached. If a puzzle is later made private
or removed, its card in the conversation shows **This puzzle is no longer
available**.

## Send photos

1. Click **Attach** (the **+** button) and choose **Photo**.
2. Pick up to 4 photos (JPEG, PNG, HEIC or WebP; HEIC and WebP work where your
   browser can open them).
3. Optionally type a caption in the message box.
4. Send.

Before a photo leaves your device, JigSwap resizes it and saves it as a new
JPEG. This removes the hidden information cameras store in photos, such as the
location where it was taken.

A puzzle and photos go in separate messages. If **Photo** is missing from the
menu, photos can't be sent right now.

### Photo checks

Every photo is checked automatically before the other member receives it. While
that happens, you see **Checking photo…** in your own view of the conversation;
the other member doesn't see anything yet.

If the check flags a photo, it isn't delivered. You see a note in the
conversation and get a **Photo not sent** notification. If you think the check
got it wrong:

1. Find the photo in the conversation.
2. Click **Ask for a review**. It changes to **Review requested**.
3. A moderator looks at it. If they restore it, the photo is delivered. If not,
   you see **Reviewed: the decision stands.**

### Blurred photos

Some photos you receive appear blurred at first:

- **Sensitive content**: the automatic check wasn't sure about the photo.
- **Not checked**: the automatic check couldn't run.
- **You don't follow each other**: the photo passed the check, but the sender
  is someone you don't follow each other with.

Click **Show** to see the photo. Once you've shown a photo, it stays visible
for you.

### View photos full size

Click a photo to open it full size. In the viewer you can:

- Swipe, or use the arrow keys, to move between photos.
- Pinch, double-tap or use the zoom buttons to zoom in, and drag to pan.
- Press **Esc** to close.
- Report a photo you think breaks the rules.

## Share a location

Use this to agree on a meet-up spot for a swap or loan.

1. Click **Attach** (the **+** button) and choose **Location**.
2. Read the note and pick a spot:
   - **Search for a place**, then choose a result, or
   - **Use my current location** (your browser asks for permission).
3. To fine-tune the pin, tap the map or use the zoom and arrow buttons.
4. Optionally add a **Name of the place** and a **Message**.
5. Click **Send location**.

How exact the shared spot is:

- A place you searched for or pinned on the map is shared exactly.
- Your current location is shared as an approximate area of about 1 km.
- If the other member is someone you follow each other with, you can turn on
  **Share my exact location** to share exactly where you are.

A location is a single spot at the moment you send it. It's never a live,
updating location. The other member sees a small map, the place name, and
**Approximate area** when it isn't exact. **Open in maps** opens the spot on a
full map in a new tab.

> **Tip:** Pick a public meet-up place, such as a station or café, rather than
> your home address.

## When you can't reply

If the message box is replaced by "You can't reply to this conversation.", the
other member is no longer reachable by you, for example because they blocked
you. If you blocked them, you see "You blocked" followed by their name, with
an **Unblock** button.

To block or report a member, or report a message or photo, see
[Blocking & Reporting](/docs/discovering-and-community/blocking-and-reporting).

> **Note:** When a member deletes their account, their text messages stay in
> your conversation, shown from **Deleted member**. Their photos and locations
> are removed.
