# Messages

**Where:** **Community → Messages** (`/messages`).

Messages is where you talk to other members: one on one about a puzzle you
spotted, a trade you're working out, or where to meet up, or together in the
group chat of a friend circle. Every exchange has its own conversation too.

## Who you can message

You can start a conversation with a member when at least one of these is true:

- You follow each other.
- You're both in the same friend circle.
- You already have a conversation or an exchange together.

If none of these apply, the **Message** button is disabled and explains
"You can only message members you're connected with". Blocking a member, in
either direction, also stops new messages between you.

## The inbox

On desktop, the inbox sits on the left and the open conversation on the right.
On a phone, you see the inbox first; opening a conversation fills the whole
screen, and the back button returns you to the inbox.

Each row in the inbox shows:

- The member's name and how long ago the last message was sent (for example
  _now_, _5m_, _2h_, a weekday, or a date).
- A one-line preview of the last message, starting with "You:" when you sent
  it.
- A count of unread messages, if there are any.
- The cover of the latest puzzle the conversation is about, when there is one.

A circle's group chat shows the circle name with a member's avatar and the
purple circle icon (two people), so you can tell it from a chat with one
person. Its preview starts with the name of whoever wrote last (for example
"Bas: Who has the Wasgij?"). A muted conversation shows a bell with a line
through it, and its unread count is grey.

## Start a conversation

**From a member's profile or their tile on People:**

1. Click **Message**.
2. JigSwap opens your conversation with that member, or starts a new one.

**About a specific puzzle:**

1. Open the puzzle copy (from **Browse** or a member's profile).
2. Click **Message**.
3. Write your first message. The puzzle is attached to it, so the owner knows
   which one you mean.
4. Send it. The conversation starts when you send the first message.

If you already have a conversation with the owner, JigSwap takes you straight
to it. You can't message about your own puzzle, and some owners keep their
profile private, in which case you can't message them from their puzzle.

**With a friend circle:**

1. Click **Open group chat** on the circle, on **Circles** or on the circle's
   own page. Or, in Messages, click **New**, choose **Circle chat** and pick
   the circle (each one shows the purple circle icon).
2. Write your message and send it.

A circle's chat shows up in your inbox once someone has written in it, or once
you've opened it.

## Circle group chats

Every friend circle has one group chat for all its members. See
[Friend Circles](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/friend-circles) for creating
circles and inviting people.

### Who can read it

- Only the circle's current members can read and write in its chat.
- When you join, you see the messages sent after you joined, not the earlier
  ones. This also applies when you rejoin: messages from while you were away
  stay hidden.
- The chat shows a short line when someone joins or leaves, such as
  "Anna joined" or "Anna is no longer in the circle". Leaving and being removed
  look the same, and the chat never says who removed someone. If you can't
  otherwise see who it was, it reads "A member joined" or "A member left".
- Each run of messages shows the writer's name and avatar. Members of the same
  circle always see each other's names.

### Members and options

Under the circle name the header says **Circle chat** and the member count.
Click the member count (for example **6 members**) to
open the members panel. It lists everyone in the circle with their role, with
a report button next to each member, plus **Mute conversation** and
**Leave circle**.

The **Conversation options** menu (**⋯**) in the header has **Mute
conversation**, **Members**, **Open circle**, **Report a member…** and
**Leave circle**. **Circle** in the header opens the circle's page.

### Mute a chat

Choose **Mute conversation** to stop all notifications from that conversation:
in-app, email and push. New messages still arrive, but the unread count is
grey and isn't added to the Messages badge. Choose **Unmute conversation**
to turn notifications back on.

### Leave or be removed

When you leave a circle, or an admin removes you:

- the chat disappears from your inbox, and you can no longer read it;
- the messages you sent stay in the chat for the other members.

Messages from someone who left stay in the chat too. You see their name only
if you could see it outside the circle, for example because you follow each
other; otherwise they show as **Anonymous user**.

The owner can't leave a circle; they can delete it, which also deletes its
chat.

### Blocking someone in a circle

Blocking a member doesn't remove either of you from a circle you share. In
that circle's chat:

- Their messages are collapsed for you as "Message from a member you blocked".
  Click **Show** to read the text; their photos stay collapsed.
- Their messages don't count as unread and don't notify you.
- They still see your messages as usual, and they aren't told about the block.
- You don't see each other typing or online.

If you'd rather not be in the group with them, you can leave the circle.

### Notifications from circle chats

- New messages in a conversation are combined into one notification, such as
  "**Anna** and 2 others wrote in your circle **Puzzle Club**."
- Push notifications for circle messages are off by default. Turn on push for
  **Circle messages** in your
  [notification preferences](/docs/staying-informed/notifications) (and enable
  push on this device) to get them. They arrive at most once every 2 minutes
  per chat, are titled **Circle:** followed by the circle's name, and don't
  show the message text.
- Circle chats never send an email per message. If you turn on email for
  **Circle messages** in your
  [notification preferences](/docs/staying-informed/notifications), you get
  one short summary about 30 minutes after a message you haven't read, at most
  once every 6 hours per chat. Email is off by default.

## See who's typing

While someone writes in a conversation you're in, a line above the message box
says so: "Anna is typing…", "Anna and Bas are typing…" or "Several people are
typing…". It disappears a few seconds after they stop, or when they send.

- Everyone in the conversation sees it, including circle members you don't
  follow each other with.
- It's never shown between you and a member you blocked or who blocked you.
- While you **Appear offline**, others don't see you typing.

## Online status

You can let mutual followers see when you're online. It's off until you choose
to share it.

**Turn it on or off:** the first time you open Messages, a card asks whether
you want to share your online status. Choose **Share** or **Not now**. You
can change this at any time with the **Online status** setting on
**Privacy & safety** (open your account menu → **Manage account** →
**Privacy & safety**).

**Who sees it:** someone can see your status only when all of these are true:

- you both share your online status;
- you follow each other;
- neither of you has blocked the other;
- you're not appearing offline.

Sharing works both ways: if you don't share yours, you don't see anyone
else's. Circle members you don't follow each other with don't see your status,
even in the circle chat. JigSwap never shows when you were last online, and
doesn't keep a history of when you were online.

**Where it shows:** a green dot on a member's avatar means online, a
half-filled dot means away. You see it on conversations in your inbox, in the
conversation header, and in a circle's members panel, where the header also
counts who's online, such as "Circle chat · 6 members · 2 online". The count includes only
members who are online (not away) and whose status you can see.

**Set your status:** open your account menu and choose your status:

- **Automatic**: you show as online while JigSwap is open, as away after
  5 minutes without activity, and as offline when the tab is hidden or closed.
- **Away**: you show as away while you're on JigSwap.
- **Appear offline**: you show as offline to everyone, and others don't see
  you typing. You can still see the status of others who share theirs.

## Read a conversation

- Messages are grouped by day, under **Today**, **Yesterday**, or the date.
- Messages one person sends within a few minutes of each other are grouped
  together, and the time shows once per group.
- When you scroll to the top, older messages load automatically. You can also
  press **Load earlier messages**. When you reach the beginning, you see
  **Start of the conversation**.

### Quick links in the header

The conversation header has shortcuts to what the conversation is about:

- **View profile** opens the other member's profile.
- **View exchange** opens the exchange, for a conversation about a trade, loan
  or sale.
- When the conversation covers two or more puzzles, a stack of covers opens a
  **Puzzles** menu listing each one and whose copy it is.

On a phone, **View profile** and **View exchange** are in the
**Conversation options** menu.

## Format your messages

You can format text in a message by typing a few characters around it. The
formatting shows in the conversation; previews in the inbox and notifications
show the plain text.

| Type this                | To get            |
| ------------------------ | ----------------- |
| `**bold**`               | **bold**          |
| `*italic*` or `_italic_` | _italic_          |
| `__underline__`          | underlined text   |
| `~~strikethrough~~`      | ~~strikethrough~~ |
| `` `code` ``             | `code`            |

- Formatting works within one line. To format several lines, mark each line.
- Characters inside a word stay as you typed them, so `snake_case` and
  `2*3*4` aren't formatted.
- To show a character such as `*` as it is, put a backslash before it: `\*`.

**Shortcuts:** select text in the message box and press **Ctrl+B** for bold,
**Ctrl+I** for italic or **Ctrl+U** for underline (**⌘** instead of **Ctrl**
on a Mac). Press the same shortcut again to remove it. Without a selection,
the shortcut adds the characters with the cursor between them.

**Links:** web addresses that start with `https://`, `http://` or `www.`
become links, which open in a new tab. The link always shows the full
address.

**Emoji:** on a computer, click the smiley button next to the message box to
pick an emoji; it's added where the cursor is. On a phone, use your
keyboard's emoji key.

## Attach a puzzle

You can point the other member at any puzzle, yours or theirs:

1. Click **Attach** (the paperclip button next to the send button).
2. Choose **Attach a puzzle**.
3. Pick from the **My puzzles** tab, or from the other member's tab (for
   example **Mara's puzzles**). Use **Search puzzles** to filter by title or
   brand.
4. The puzzle appears above the message box. Write your message and send it.

You only see puzzles that both of you are allowed to see. A message always
needs some text, even with a puzzle attached. If a puzzle is later made private
or removed, its card in the conversation shows **This puzzle is no longer
available**.

### In a circle chat

In a circle's group chat you can attach one of your own puzzles for the
members who can see it:

- The picker has only the **My puzzles** tab, and lists the puzzles at least
  one other member can see. The whole circle sees puzzles you've
  [shared with the circle](/docs/sharing-and-exchanges/friend-circles#share-a-copy-with-a-circle)
  while you offer them for swap, lend or sale, or, if your profile is public,
  any puzzle you offer. Members of your household, and members you follow
  who follow you back, can also see your other puzzles (household members
  even your private ones).
- A puzzle that only some members can see is marked **Not everyone in the
  circle can see this** in the picker. You can still attach it.
- You can't attach another member's puzzle in a circle chat.
- Each member sees the card only while they're allowed to see the puzzle.
  Members who can't, for example because they aren't linked to you or because
  you removed the puzzle, stopped sharing or offering it, or left the circle,
  see **This puzzle isn't available to you** instead. It shows nothing about
  the puzzle. A member who blocked you, or whom you blocked, sees that too.

## Send photos

1. Click **Attach** (the paperclip button) and choose **Photo**.
2. Pick up to 4 photos (JPEG, PNG, HEIC or WebP; HEIC and WebP work where your
   browser can open them).
3. Optionally type a caption in the message box.
4. Send.

Before a photo leaves your device, JigSwap resizes it and saves it as a new
JPEG. This removes the hidden information cameras store in photos, such as the
location where it was taken. Some browsers block this step to protect your
privacy (for example Firefox with fingerprinting protection turned on). Then
the original photo is sent, and JigSwap removes that hidden information on its
servers before anyone can see the photo. Such a photo must be a JPEG, PNG or
WebP of at most 10 MB.

A puzzle and photos go in separate messages. If **Photo** is missing from the
menu, photos can't be sent right now.

### Photo checks

Every photo is checked automatically before the other member receives it. While
that happens, you see **Checking photo…** in your own view of the conversation;
the other member doesn't see anything yet.

If the check flags a photo, it isn't delivered. You see a note in the
conversation and get a **Photo not sent** notification. If you think the check
got it wrong:

1. Find the photo in the conversation.
2. Click **Ask for a review**. It changes to **Review requested**.
3. A moderator looks at it. If they restore it, the photo is delivered. If not,
   you see **Reviewed: the decision stands.**

### Blurred photos

Some photos you receive appear blurred at first:

- **Sensitive content**: the automatic check wasn't sure about the photo.
- **Not checked**: the automatic check couldn't run.
- **You don't follow each other**: the photo passed the check, but the sender
  is someone you don't follow each other with.

Click **Show** to see the photo. Once you've shown a photo, it stays visible
for you.

### View photos full size

Click a photo to open it full size. In the viewer you can:

- Swipe, or use the arrow keys, to move between photos.
- Pinch, double-tap or use the zoom buttons to zoom in, and drag to pan.
- Press **Esc** to close.
- Report a photo you think breaks the rules.

## Share a location

Use this to agree on a meet-up spot for a swap or loan.

1. Click **Attach** (the paperclip button) and choose **Location**.
2. Read the note and pick a spot:
   - **Search for a place**, then choose a result, or
   - **Use my current location** (your browser asks for permission).
3. To fine-tune the pin, tap the map or use the zoom and arrow buttons.
4. Optionally add a **Name of the place** and a **Message**.
5. Click **Send location**.

How exact the shared spot is:

- A place you searched for or pinned on the map is shared exactly.
- Your current location is shared as an approximate area of about 1 km.
- If the other member is someone you follow each other with, you can turn on
  **Share my exact location** to share exactly where you are.

A location is a single spot at the moment you send it. It's never a live,
updating location. The other member sees a small map, the place name, and
**Approximate area** when it isn't exact. **Open in maps** opens the spot on a
full map in a new tab.

> **Tip:** Pick a public meet-up place, such as a station or café, rather than
> your home address.

## When you can't reply

If the message box is replaced by "You can't reply to this conversation.", the
other member is no longer reachable by you, for example because they blocked
you. If you blocked them, you see "You blocked" followed by their name, with
an **Unblock** button.

To block or report a member, or report a message or photo, see
[Blocking & Reporting](/docs/discovering-and-community/blocking-and-reporting).

> **Note:** When a member deletes their account, their text messages stay in
> your conversation, shown from **Deleted member**. Their photos and locations
> are removed.
