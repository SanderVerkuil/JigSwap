# Notifications

**Where:** **Notifications** (`/notifications`), or the bell in the top bar.

JigSwap notifies you about exchanges, messages, followers, households and
circles, goal achievements, reviews, your catalogue submissions, and the
outcome of reports and moderation.

## Reading notifications

Each notification names who did something and what it was about, for example
"**Mara** wants to trade for **Cats in the Garden**." Where it helps, it also
shows extra context: a preview of a message, a star rating, a reason, or the
current status of a trade.

- The **bell** shows your latest notifications. **See all notifications**
  opens the full page.
- Unread notifications are highlighted. Click one to open what it's about; it's
  marked as read automatically.
- Use **Mark as read** on a single notification, or **Mark all as read** for
  all of them.
- The Notifications page groups notifications under **Today**, **Yesterday**
  and **Earlier**. Filter with **All** or **Unread only**, and press
  **Load more** at the bottom to see older notifications.
- When several people follow you around the same time, they're combined into
  one notification, such as "**Mara**, **Joost** and 3 others followed you."
- A trade notification shows the trade's current status. Clicking it opens that
  exchange on the **Exchanges** page.
- If what a notification refers to is gone, it shows **No longer available**.

## Act from a notification

Some notifications let you respond without leaving the list. The bell offers
the follow and household actions; the Notifications page offers all of them.

| Notification                           | What you can do                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Someone followed you                   | **Follow back**. A short **Undo** appears in case you clicked by mistake.                                                                                      |
| Someone asked to follow you            | **Approve** or **Decline**. On the Notifications page you can also **Approve & follow back**, and open **View profile** to see who's asking before you decide. |
| Your follow request was approved       | **Follow back**.                                                                                                                                               |
| Someone invited you to their household | **Accept** or **Decline**.                                                                                                                                     |
| You received a message                 | **Reply** (on the Notifications page).                                                                                                                         |

After you act, the buttons are replaced by the result, such as **Following**,
**You follow each other**, **Approved** or **Joined**.

## Email and push

Email and push notifications carry the same details as the in-app ones: who
did it, which puzzle or trade it's about, and a link straight to it. A push
notification for a new message shows a preview of the message, except in a
circle's group chat.

## Notification preferences

**Where:** **Notifications → Notification preferences**
(`/notifications/preferences`).

Notification types are grouped into categories: **Trades**, **Messages**,
**Social**, **My submissions**, **Product updates** and **Safety** (admins
also see **Admin**). Use **Jump to** to go to a category.

- Turn each notification type on or off for each channel: **In-app**,
  **Email** and **Push**.
- By default, every type is on for **In-app**; email and push are off until
  you turn them on.
- Email isn't offered for every type.
- A few safety notices, such as when a moderator removes your content or warns
  you, are marked **Always on: legally required notices**. They always appear
  in-app; you can still choose email and push for them.
- Under **Push on this device** in the same screen, click **Enable** to get
  push notifications in this browser (your browser asks for permission).

> **Tip:** On iPhone and iPad, push notifications only work after you add
> JigSwap to your home screen. See
> [Finding Your Way Around](/docs/getting-started/finding-your-way-around).

## Notification types you may see

Besides trades, messages, reviews, goals and your catalogue submissions, these
notifications cover newer features:

- **Invitations to follow each other**: the person whose invite link you
  signed up with asks whether you two should follow each other. See
  [People & Following](/docs/discovering-and-community/people-and-following).
- **Circle invites** and **Circle invite accepted**: someone invites you to a
  friend circle, or accepts your invitation.
- **Circle deleted**: the owner deleted a circle you were in.
- **Trade cancelled (account deleted)**: a trade you had under way was
  cancelled because the other member deleted their account. They show as
  **Deleted member**.
- **Circle messages**: someone wrote in the group chat of one of your circles.
  Messages in one conversation are combined into one notification. See
  [Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages#notifications-from-circle-chats).
- **Report outcomes**: a moderator decided on a report you filed.
- **Content removed** and **Moderator warnings**: a moderator acted on one of
  your messages or photos after a report.
- **Photos not sent**: the automatic check stopped a photo you sent in a
  message. See [Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages).
- **What's new**: a new feature or change to JigSwap.
