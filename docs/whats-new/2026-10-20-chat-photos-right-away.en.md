title: "Photos in chats arrive right away"
summary: "Photos you send now appear in the conversation straight away, in the order you sent them, marked Pending review until they've been checked."
category: improvement
---

Photos you send in a conversation now appear straight away, in the order you
sent them. They no longer turn up later, after the conversation has moved on.

- **The other member** sees a blurred placeholder marked **Pending review**
  until the photo has passed the automatic check. Then the photo appears in
  the same place.
- **You** see your own photo with **Checking photo…** in the meantime.
- **If the check stops a photo**, the other member sees **Removed by
  moderation** and never sees the photo itself. You can still ask for a
  review.
- **If the check can't run**, a moderator looks at the photo, instead of it
  being shown blurred as "Not checked".
