- **Privacy** on your profile shows who can see you and saves straight away,
  with **Undo**. A badge says whether your profile is public or private, next
  to **View public profile**.
- **Edit Profile** fits on a phone screen, with every switch in reach.
- **Unfollow** is under the **Following** button, with **Undo**.
- **Notifications** are grouped into today, yesterday, this week and this
  month, and on a phone the filters fit in one row.
- **People:** activity names the puzzle, and several puzzles someone added on
  one day share one row.
