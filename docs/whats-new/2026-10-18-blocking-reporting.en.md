You now have more control over who can reach you, and a way to tell us when
something isn't right.

## Block a member

Open the menu on their profile or in a conversation and choose to block them
(for example **Block Sam**). They can no longer message you, follow you or
propose trades, and you stop following each other. They aren't told, but
they'll see they can't reply. Trades you already accepted can still be
completed, and shared circles and households don't change.

## Report something

Use **Report message**, **Report photo**, **Report member** on a trade, or
report someone from their profile (for example **Report Sam**). Pick a reason,
add details if you like, and block them in the same step. Reports are private:
only our moderators see them, and the member won't know who reported them.
We'll let you know what we decided.

## Privacy & safety

Open your account menu, choose **Manage account** and then **Privacy &
safety** to see the members you've blocked and unblock them. Unblocking doesn't
bring back the follows the block removed.
