title: "Tours let you press the button they point at"
summary: "When a tour step asks you to press a button, you can now press it and the tour carries on."
category: fix
---

Some tour steps highlight a button you need to press, such as **Create
household** in the households tour. The tour used to block that button, so
you got stuck.

Now those steps say "Press the highlighted button to continue." Press it and
the tour moves on by itself. Back and close still work as usual.
