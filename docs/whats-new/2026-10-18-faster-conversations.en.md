Messages and your inbox now load faster, however long a conversation gets.

- **Latest first.** A conversation opens on its 20 most recent messages.
- **Scroll up for more.** Older messages load as you scroll back, and the page
  keeps your place. You can also use **Load earlier messages**.
- **Start of the conversation** tells you when there's nothing older.
