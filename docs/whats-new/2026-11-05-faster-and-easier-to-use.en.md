title: "Faster, and easier for everyone"
summary: "Pages download about a third less code, pictures are much smaller, and JigSwap works better with a keyboard, a screen reader, a touch screen and in Dutch."
category: improvement
---

- **Faster first load:** most pages download about a third less code, and the
  docs load one page at a time.
- **Smaller pictures:** box art and photos come in a size that fits the card.
  On a phone, the catalogue's first screen went from 3.2 MB to under 0.5 MB.
- **The 3D shelf** loads only on devices that can handle it, and its physics
  only when you press Play.
- **Easier to read:** text meets the WCAG AA contrast level in light and dark
  mode.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** a skip link, one main heading per page, page
  changes announced, and focus that goes back where it was when a dialog
  closes.
- **Touch:** buttons are at least 44 pixels, and text fields no longer make an
  iPhone zoom in.
- **Your language:** numbers and dates follow the app's language, and the Dutch
  wording is the same everywhere.
