Phones store hidden details in every photo, such as the place it was taken.
JigSwap now removes them from every photo you add, wherever you add it.

## What's changed

- **Every photo, every place.** Photos of your puzzles and solves, box art and
  photos in messages lose their location, camera and date details. Only the
  orientation stays, so photos still show the right way up.
- **Checked on our servers too.** Your browser removes the details before
  uploading where it can. JigSwap's servers remove anything left before anyone
  else can see the photo.
- **Privacy-focused browsers.** Browsers such as Brave, Helium or Firefox with
  fingerprinting protection used to upload the original photo, or refuse it in
  messages. Photos now work there too, without the hidden details.
