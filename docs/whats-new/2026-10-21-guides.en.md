Tours used to live on the update that introduced a feature, so they were hard
to find again later. Now they're all on the **Guides** tab of this page.

- **Grouped by area**, like your puzzles, sharing and solving.
- **See where you are.** A guide you stopped partway shows the step you
  reached; a finished one is marked **Done**.
- **Take, resume or replay** any guide with one click.
- **New guides** for features that never had a tour: adding your puzzles,
  swapping, borrowing and buying, who sees what, friend circles, tracking
  your solves and improving the catalogue.
- **Press ? in the page header** on Exchanges, Circles, Completions and Add
  Puzzle to start that page's guide right there.
