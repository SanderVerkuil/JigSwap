summary: "Circle members see each other's names, private members stay unlinkable, and traded puzzles keep their new owner's details private."
category: fix
---

Another round of work so the app does what your settings say:

- **Circle members see each other's names.** Being in the same circle shows
  who the other members are, but a private profile, library and activity stay
  private. Leave the circle and that ends.
- **Private owners stay private.** If a puzzle's owner keeps their profile
  private, you can't message them from the puzzle's page. Members shown as
  anonymous can't be linked to each other across pages.
- **Traded puzzles leave your collections.** When a puzzle changes owner, it's
  removed from the previous owner's collections. Personal details such as
  notes and the purchase price are only shown to the current owner.
