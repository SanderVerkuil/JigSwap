Messages has a new look and a few new tricks:

- **A compact inbox.** Every conversation is one tidy row with a short time
  ("5m", "2h", "Oct 12") and, when it's about a puzzle, that puzzle's cover.
- **Easier-to-read conversations.** Messages are split by day (Today,
  Yesterday, then the date), and messages sent close together are grouped.
- **Full screen on phones.** An open conversation fills the screen and the
  composer stays above the keyboard.
- **Quick links.** The conversation header has **View profile** and, for a
  trade, **View exchange**. When a conversation is about several puzzles, tap
  their covers to see the whole list.
- **Attach a puzzle.** Use **Attach a puzzle** in the composer to add one of
  your puzzles, or one of theirs, to any message.
