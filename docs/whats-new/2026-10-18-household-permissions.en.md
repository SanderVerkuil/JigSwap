You no longer have to open puzzles one by one to change their household
access.

- **Bulk edit.** On the Household page, _My puzzles in this household_ lists
  your puzzles with their current access. Filter them, tick the ones you want
  (or **Select all**) and choose **Set to Can manage** or **Set to View only**.
- **A household default.** Under _Household settings_, the household admin
  picks Can manage or View only for every puzzle whose owner hasn't chosen
  otherwise. Everyone else sees the setting there.
- **Use the default, or pin a choice.** On a puzzle's page, or in the bulk
  editor, pick _Use household default_ to follow the household setting, or set
  Can manage or View only to keep that puzzle as it is whatever the default
  becomes.
