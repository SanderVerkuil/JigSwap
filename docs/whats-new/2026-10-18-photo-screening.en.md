title: "Photos are checked before others see them"
summary: "New photos are reviewed before they appear for other members. Until then, they see a blurred placeholder."
category: improvement
---

To keep JigSwap a friendly place, every photo you add to a puzzle or a solve is
checked before other members can see it.

- **You see your photo right away**, marked _Pending review_.
- **Other members see a blurred placeholder** with the same label until the
  check is done. It shows only soft colours, never the picture itself.
- **Covers** only ever use photos that have passed the check.
