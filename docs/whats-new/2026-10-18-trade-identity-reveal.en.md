To arrange a swap you need to know who's on the other side. Each trade on
Exchanges now shows a trade-partner card with their name, handle, email
address, rating and the reviews they've received.

## How it works

- **When you send a request**, the owner of the puzzle sees your details for
  that trade. The request dialog tells you this before you send.
- **The owner's details appear** once they accept or reply in the trade's
  conversation. Until then the card says so. If they decline without
  replying, they stay anonymous.
- **Only within that trade.** It never opens up the other member's profile,
  library or activity, and a new trade between the same two members starts
  hidden again. If your profile is private, it stays private.
