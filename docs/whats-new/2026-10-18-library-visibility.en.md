Your profile used to list only the puzzles you offer to swap, lend or sell.
Now the people closest to you can see your whole shelf.

## Who sees what

- **Mutual followers and your household** see your whole library on your
  profile, with an **All puzzles** / **Available** filter.
- **Everyone else** still sees only the puzzles you offer to swap, lend or
  sell.
- **Your puzzles are visible unless you choose otherwise**, including the ones
  you already have.

## Keep a puzzle private

Open the puzzle, choose **Edit**, and under _Who can see this copy_ pick
**Private — only you and your household**. A private puzzle is hidden from
everyone else, friends included. Only you, your household and whoever is
holding it (for example while it's lent out) can see it, plus the other member
of a trade it's part of. Private puzzles carry a **Private** badge.

A puzzle you offer for swap, lend or sale is always visible. To make it
private, withdraw the offer first.
