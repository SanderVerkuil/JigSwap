The **+** button in the message composer now does more than attach a puzzle.

## Photos

- **Up to 4 photos at a time**, with an optional caption. Hidden details such
  as the location stored in a photo are removed on your device before upload.
- **Checked first.** Each photo is checked before the other member receives
  it. If the check stops a photo, it isn't sent and you can **Ask for a
  review**.
- **You decide what to see.** Photos from someone you don't follow each other
  with, or marked _Sensitive content_ or _Not checked_, arrive blurred. Tap
  **Show** to see them.

## Locations

- **Share a meeting spot.** Choose **Location**, search for a place or **Use my
  current location**, and send it. It's a single spot, never your live
  position, and the other member can **Open in maps**.
- **Approximate by default.** Your current location is shared as an area of
  about 1 km. Only with a mutual follower can you choose **Share my exact
  location**. Pick a public meet-up spot rather than your home address.
