The cards on People (Your network, Find people and Recently joined) have a new layout:

- **Room for the name.** Long names wrap onto two lines instead of being cut
  off.
- **Click anywhere** on the card to open the profile. Middle-click and
  ⌘/Ctrl-click open it in a new tab.
- **Compact actions.** Follow and Message are icon buttons with a tooltip, and
  they grow to a comfortable size on touch screens.
- **Honest ratings.** A member without reviews shows "No reviews yet" instead
  of an empty star.
