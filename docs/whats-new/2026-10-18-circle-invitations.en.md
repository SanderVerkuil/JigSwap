Joining a circle is now always your choice.

- **Invite, don't add.** Circle admins choose **Invite** and pick from the
  people they follow mutually. An invite expires after 14 days, and admins can
  revoke it while it's pending.
- **Accept or decline.** You get a notification, and your invitations appear
  under _Invitations_ on the Circles page, with who invited you. Until you
  accept, you're not a member and nothing is shared with you.
- **Every admin can manage.** Not just the owner: any circle admin can invite,
  manage members and share puzzles.
- **Delete a circle.** The owner can delete a circle by typing its name to
  confirm. It's removed for everyone and its members get a notification.
  Shared puzzles stay in their owners' libraries.
- **A name limit.** New circle names can be up to 100 characters.

Members and pending invites together count toward the limit of 50.
