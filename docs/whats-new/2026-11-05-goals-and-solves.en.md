title: "Goals and solves you can trust"
summary: "Goals count only the solves inside their period, and you can edit, archive or delete them. Logging a solve is quicker, and Insights is easier to read."
category: improvement
---

- **Goals** count only the solves you finish inside their period. You can edit
  a goal, archive it, restore it or delete it, and reaching a target is
  announced once.
- **Log a solve** lists the puzzles you're solving first, includes borrowed and
  household puzzles, and works fully from the keyboard.
- **Your solve log** uses the same dates everywhere, shows the times you typed
  and has a **Finished** / **Still solving** switch.
- **Insights:** the charts have text descriptions, quiet months show up, the
  difficulty bar has a legend, and **Export my data** is easier to find.
