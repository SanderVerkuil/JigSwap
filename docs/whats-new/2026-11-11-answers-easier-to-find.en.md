- **Search opens at the answer.** Each result says where it is, such as
  Messages › Blurred photos, and shows the words that matched. Words like "how"
  and "my" no longer flood the results.
- **The FAQ** has six groups with an **On this page** list, and new answers:
  offering your own puzzle, giving back a borrowed puzzle, getting a lent puzzle
  back, fixing a catalogue puzzle's details, a forgotten password and deleting
  your account.
- **Answers match the app.** A few guides named buttons the app doesn't have,
  or sent you the wrong way. They're fixed, in English and Dutch.
- **Help & guides** is in your account menu and, on a phone, in the + menu.
  Press **?** in the header of the dashboard or People to take that page's
  guide.
- **What's new** shows short summaries with **Read more**, and you can filter
  updates by type.
