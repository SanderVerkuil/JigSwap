title: "Circle permissions now do what they say"
summary: "View only members see a circle's shared puzzles but can't request them. Can exchange members can request them and share their own puzzles too."
category: improvement
---

The permission you give someone in a circle now decides what they can do with
the puzzles shared there.

- **View only** members see the shared puzzles, but can't send a request for
  them. The copy page tells them why.
- **Can exchange** and **Admin** members can request shared puzzles, as
  before.
- **Can exchange** members can now share their own puzzles into the circle,
  from the circle's page under **Shared puzzles**. Before, only admins could.
- The invite picker and the manage dialog show a one-line summary under each
  level.

Someone who also reaches a puzzle another way, for example because you follow
each other, can still request it.
