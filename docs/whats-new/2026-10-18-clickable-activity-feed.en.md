The activity feed, on the Activity tab of People and on your dashboard, is now
clickable:

- **Names** open the member's profile.
- **The entry itself** opens what happened: the puzzle someone added or
  finished, the puzzles on their profile when they added several, or the trade
  on Exchanges when you were part of it.

Entries only link to pages you can open. Anything you can't see stays plain
text.
