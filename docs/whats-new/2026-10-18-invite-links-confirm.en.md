Following someone should be a choice. So when a friend signs up through your
invite link, you no longer follow each other straight away.

- **They get asked.** The new member gets a notification, and on their
  dashboard and on People they see that you invited them, with the question
  "Follow each other?" and the buttons **Follow each other** and **Not now**.
  The question stays open for 30 days.
- **You hear when they say yes.** Once they accept, you get the usual new
  follower notification.
- **Household links still work** the same way: the household invite waits for
  them to accept, separately from following.

Following each other in person with a QR code hasn't changed.
