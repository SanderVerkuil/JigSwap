title: "Zoom in on puzzle photos"
summary: "Pinch, double-tap or use the zoom buttons to get a closer look at a puzzle's photos."
category: improvement
---

When you open a puzzle's photo, you can now get up close:

- **Pinch** to zoom up to 4x, and **drag** to look around.
- **Double-tap** to zoom in on that spot, and again to zoom back out.
- **Zoom buttons** do the same with a mouse or keyboard.

Swiping to the next photo works as before when the photo is at normal size.
While you're zoomed in, a swipe moves around the photo instead.
