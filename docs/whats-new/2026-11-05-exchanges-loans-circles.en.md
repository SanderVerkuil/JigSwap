- **What waits on you comes first** on Exchanges. Each row shows who has
  confirmed, the price offered and when a lent puzzle came back.
- **Every action answers back:** you see that it worked, or why it didn't.
- **Either of you can cancel an accepted exchange.** JigSwap asks you first and
  tells the other member.
- **Loans** has its own tab on Exchanges, with the puzzles you've borrowed and
  the ones you've lent out. The old Borrowed page takes you there. You can start
  a solve on a borrowed puzzle from its page, and lenders can message the
  borrower.
- **Circles:** share or unshare many puzzles from one searchable list, or from
  the puzzle itself. You can see your own permission in each circle, and admins
  can invite someone from that member's profile.
- **Household:** the explanation is on every tab, and the bulk controls only
  show while you've selected something.
