title: "More say over what you wrote and who sees you"
summary: "You can delete your own comments and reviews. A private profile now hides your name in reviews and comments too. A block reaches your household as well."
category: improvement
---

- **Delete what you wrote:** your puzzle comments, photo comments and reviews
  each have a delete option. On a review of an exchange partner you can remove
  the written comment; the stars stay.
- **Private stays private:** members you're not connected with now see you as
  **Anonymous user** in reviews, comments, the activity feed and collections
  too. Mutual followers, members of a friend circle you share and the other
  member in a trade still see your name.
- **Blocks reach the household:** a block between housemates ends their
  managing of each other's puzzles, and someone blocked by a member of a
  household can't join it.
- **Household invites** expire after 14 days, and are withdrawn when the person
  who sent them leaves the household.
- **Signing out** reloads the page, so the next person on that device sees
  nothing of yours.
- **Your data export** names the same people the app shows you.
