JigSwap now looks like a street of houses with the fronts off, so you can see
everyone's shelf. Only the look is new: your puzzles, settings and messages are
where you left them.

- **New colours:** sky blue, canal-house green and marigold replace the purple.
- **New fonts:** Young Serif for headings and Hanken Grotesk for text.
- **The app** sits in a sky-blue frame, with a drawn skyline at the foot of the
  sidebar. In dark mode its windows light up.
- **The home page** shows a house cut open: click a floor to see that person's
  shelf. **How it works** follows one puzzle box from shelf to shelf.
- **Help and the legal pages** stay calm and plain, so they're easy to read.
