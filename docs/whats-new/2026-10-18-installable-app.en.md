JigSwap can now be installed on your phone and opened like any other app.

- **On Android**, tap **Install app** when the tip appears. No button? Open
  Chrome's **⋮** menu and choose **Add to home screen** or **Install app**.
- **In Firefox on Android**, the tip shows the steps: open Firefox's **⋮** menu
  and choose **Add app to Home screen** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **On iPhone and iPad**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Installing
  also lets JigSwap send you notifications there. In Firefox, the **Share**
  button is in the address bar or Firefox's menu.

The tip shows up on your phone from your second visit. Missed it or turned it
off? Tap the **+** button in the bottom bar and choose **Install app**.
