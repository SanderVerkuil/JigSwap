title: "Give your circle an icon"
summary: "Circle admins can upload one icon for a circle. Members see it on the circle's card, its page and its group chat."
category: feature
---

A circle can now have its own icon, so you can tell your circles and their
group chats apart at a glance.

- **Admins** set it in the manage dialog under **Icon**, or with **Change
  icon** in the group chat's menu. Pick a JPEG, PNG or WebP image and crop it
  to a square.
- Members see the icon on **Circles**, on the circle's page and next to its
  group chat, with a small circle badge so it never looks like a chat with one
  person.
- Every icon goes through the same automatic check as other photos. Members
  see a new icon once it is approved; until then they keep the previous one.
- Admins can remove the icon at any time to go back to the usual picture.
