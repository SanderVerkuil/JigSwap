Reviews no longer belong to a single solve. There are now two kinds:

- **A puzzle review**: one rating and optional text per member and puzzle,
  shown on the puzzle's catalogue page.
- **A copy rating**: stars for the physical copy you solved, shown on the copy
  page. You can rate a copy you own or one you've completed.

Solve the same puzzle again and the review form opens with what you wrote
before, so you update your review rather than adding another. Every rating on
JigSwap now counts one voice per member, so some totals are a little lower
than before.
