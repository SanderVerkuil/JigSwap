Messages now works for groups, and conversations feel more live.

## Circle group chats

- **Every friend circle has a group chat.** Click **Open group chat** on a
  circle, or choose **New** → **Circle chat** in Messages.
- **Only current members can read it.** New members see messages sent after
  they joined, not the earlier ones. If you leave or are removed, the chat
  disappears from your inbox, and your messages stay in the group.
- **Mute a busy chat.** Choose **Mute conversation** in the conversation menu
  to stop its notifications.
- **A page for each circle.** Open a circle to see its members, the latest
  messages and the puzzles shared in it.

## Typing indicators

While someone writes in a conversation you're in, you see "Anna is typing…"
above the message box. This works in every conversation, including circle
chats.

## Online status

- **Your choice.** Online status is off until you choose to share it. If you
  don't share yours, you don't see anyone else's.
- **Mutual followers only.** Only people you follow each other with, who also
  share their status, can see whether you're online or away.
- **Set your status.** Pick **Automatic**, **Away** or **Appear offline** from
  your account menu. JigSwap never shows when you were last online.

## Notifications

- **One notification per conversation.** New messages are grouped, for example
  "Anna and 2 others wrote in Puzzle Club."
- **Circle chat email is a digest.** If you turn email on for **Circle
  messages**, you get one short summary about 30 minutes after a new message
  you haven't read, never an email per message. It's off by default.
