A round of fixes behind the scenes, so the app does what your settings say:

- **Private profiles stay private everywhere.** If you keep your profile
  private, members you aren't connected with can't tell it's you in messages,
  notifications, trades or the activity feed.
- **Circle shares follow the owner.** When a puzzle changes owner, or its owner
  leaves a circle, it's no longer shared in that circle. A puzzle is only
  reachable through the circle it was actually shared into.
- **Analytics wait for your consent.** The analytics tool only loads after you
  accept it in the cookie notice.
