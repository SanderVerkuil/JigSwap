summary: "When a page's tabs don't fit on your screen, you can now scroll them sideways, and the tab bar stays at the top while you scroll the page."
category: fix
---

On a narrow screen some tab bars, like the one on **People**, were wider than
the screen, and the last tabs couldn't be reached.

- **Swipe or scroll the tabs sideways.** A mouse wheel works too.
- **A soft fade** at the edge shows there are more tabs.
- **The tab you're on** always scrolls into view.
- **The tab bar stays put.** On **People**, **Household** and the other pages
  with tabs, it stays just below the top bar while you scroll, so you can
  switch tabs without scrolling back up.
