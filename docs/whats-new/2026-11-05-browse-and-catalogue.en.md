- **The brand filter works again**, also with puzzles that have no brand. Each
  filter loads on its own, so one problem doesn't empty the others.
- **Search and filters** cover the whole catalogue, and the count says how many
  puzzles match.
- **Browse Puzzles** filters by availability across every listing, shows the
  exact total and has **Load more**. One member with many listings no longer
  pushes everyone else to later pages.
- **Going back** returns you to the same search, filters, view and scroll
  position.
- **Empty results** say why they're empty, and the add buttons say where the
  puzzle ends up.
