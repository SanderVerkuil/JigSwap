Notifications used to say "Someone started following you." Now they say who,
and you can often act on them right away.

- **Who and what.** Each notification names the member and, where it fits,
  the puzzle it's about.
- **Follow back** in one tap, with an **Undo** if you change your mind.
- **Follow requests** can be approved or declined from the notification. On
  the Notifications page, **View profile** shows a short preview of who's
  asking first.
- **Household invites** can be accepted or declined in place.
- **New followers are grouped**, for example "Mara, Joost and 3 others
  followed you."
- **Trades** show their current status and open that trade on Exchanges.
- **Load more** on the Notifications page takes you further back.

Emails and push notifications got the same treatment: they name the member and
the puzzle, and open the exact conversation or trade.
