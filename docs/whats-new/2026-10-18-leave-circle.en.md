Circles are for swapping with people you know, and sometimes that changes.
Members can now step out of a circle themselves.

- **Choose Leave circle** on the circle and confirm. You lose access to the
  circle and the puzzles shared in it. An admin can invite you again later.
- **Your puzzles leave with you.** Anything you shared into that circle is no
  longer shared there. Your other circles aren't affected.
- **The owner stays.** A circle's owner can't leave it.

Circles now hold up to 50 members, including the owner.
