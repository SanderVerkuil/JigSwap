The **+** button in the message composer now does more than attach a puzzle.

## Photos

- **Send up to 4 photos at a time**, with an optional caption. JPEG, PNG, HEIC
  and WebP work. Photos are resized on your device, and hidden details such as
  the location stored in a photo are removed before upload.
- **Checked first.** Like other photos on JigSwap, each photo is checked
  before the other member receives it. It shows _Checking photo…_ until the
  check is done. If the check stops a photo, it isn't sent and you can **Ask
  for a review**.
- **You decide what to see.** A photo from someone you don't follow each other
  with, one marked _Sensitive content_, or one the check couldn't finish (_Not
  checked_) arrives blurred. Tap **Show** to see it.
- **Tap a photo** to open it full screen and zoom in.

## Locations

Choose **Location**, search for a place or **Use my current location**, and
send it. It's a single spot, never your live position. The other member sees
it on a map with **Open in maps**. Your current location goes as an
approximate area of about 1 km, unless you share it with a mutual follower and
choose **Share my exact location**. Pick a public meet-up spot rather than
your home address.

The map is made on our servers, so the map service never sees your IP address
or the other member's.
