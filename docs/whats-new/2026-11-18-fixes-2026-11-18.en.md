- **Pinch to zoom** on a puzzle's cover zooms the picture instead of the whole
  page.
- **Reading a conversation** clears its message notification, and the bell no
  longer quotes your own reply.
- **A puzzle's average solve time** is right again for times you typed in.
- **Solves on a profile** are in the order you finished them.
- **The same barcode** with or without a leading 0 counts as one puzzle.
- **Importing from a shop link** finds the box picture more often.
- **A turned-down puzzle submission** always comes with the admin's reason.
- **Leaving a form while it saves** still asks first, so a failed save doesn't
  lose your work.
- **"Was this page helpful?"** in the docs starts fresh on every page, and the
  contact form shows how long your name and message can be.
