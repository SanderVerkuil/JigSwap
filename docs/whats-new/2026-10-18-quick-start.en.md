New members now get a short **Quick setup** to get going. Most of it helps
everyone, so here's what you can use today:

- **Add several puzzles at once.** On the Add Puzzle page, choose **Add
  several**, tick up to 25 puzzles from the catalogue and add them in one go.
  You can mark each one as available to swap.
- **Better search when adding.** The search now covers the whole catalogue.
  Type a 12- or 13-digit barcode to look a puzzle up directly, or register it
  if it isn't there yet.
- **Log past solves in bulk.** Open **Quick setup** from your account menu and
  go to _Log puzzles you finished_. Tick the puzzles you've done and, for each,
  pick a date, just a year, or "I don't remember".
- **Invite housemates with a link.** The household admin can choose **Invite
  with a link** on the Household page and send it with your phone's share
  sheet, email or WhatsApp. The link works once and expires in 14 days. JigSwap
  doesn't see or store who you send it to.
