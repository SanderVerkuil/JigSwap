The Household page used to show everything at once. It's now split up so you
find what you came for straight away.

## What's changed

- **Every puzzle in the household first.** The page opens on **Puzzles**:
  every puzzle in your household, yours and your housemates', each showing
  whose it is. Narrow it down to one person, or to puzzles for trade or lend.
  In **My Puzzles**, a housemate's puzzle now shows their picture and name,
  and you can show only yours or only theirs.
- **Four tabs.** Next to **Puzzles**, **Members** shows who's in your
  household and who's still invited. **Sharing** is where you choose what your
  household may do with each of your puzzles. **Settings** holds the household
  default and the option to leave.
- **One Invite button.** Household admins find every way to invite in one
  place: search for a member, share a link, or send an email.
- **All pending invitations in one list.** The **Members** tab lists every
  invitation still waiting for an answer, in the app and by email, and you
  can revoke any of them.
- **A name for your household.** The admin can name the household on the
  **Settings** tab. Only its members see the name.
- **Switch households in one step.** Already in a household and invited to
  another? Choose **Leave and join**: you see exactly what happens to your
  current household before you confirm.
- **Invitations first.** If someone invited you and you're not in a household
  yet, their invitation is the first thing you see.
