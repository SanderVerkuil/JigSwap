The Household page used to show everything at once. It's now split up so you
find what you came for straight away.

## What's changed

- **Three tabs.** **Members** shows who's in your household and who's still
  invited. **My puzzles** is where you choose what your household may do with
  each of your puzzles. **Settings** holds the household default and the option
  to leave.
- **One Invite button.** Household admins find every way to invite in one
  place: search for a member, share a link, or send an email.
- **Invitations first.** If someone invited you and you're not in a household
  yet, their invitation is the first thing you see.
