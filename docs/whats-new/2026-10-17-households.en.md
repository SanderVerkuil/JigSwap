Living with other puzzlers? A household pools your libraries without
changing who owns what.

## How it works

- **Create a household** on the Household page and invite members by
  searching for them. They join once they accept. You can be in one
  household at a time.
- **Everyone sees everyone's puzzles** in My Puzzles, badged with the owner's
  name.
- **You choose per puzzle.** On a puzzle's page, set _Household access_ to
  **Can manage** (housemates can edit, lend and swap it) or **View only**.
  New puzzles start at Can manage.
- **Some things stay yours.** Only the owner can delete a puzzle, change its
  household access, or add it to circles, collections and their shelf.
- **Solves stay personal.** Anyone in the household can start and log their
  own solves on a shared puzzle.

When you leave a household, your puzzles go with you.
