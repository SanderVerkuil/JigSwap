title: "Messages pick up where you left off"
summary: "A conversation opens at a New messages line, and only what you've seen counts as read. You can mute a conversation with a member, and search and filter a long inbox."
category: improvement
---

- **New messages** marks where you stopped reading, and only messages you've
  seen count as read.
- **Message a member** under **New** opens the member picker right there.
- **Mute** a conversation with a member, as you already could a circle chat.
- **Search and filter** your inbox by people, exchanges or circles.
- **More room on tablets.** Messages splits into a list and a conversation based
  on the space it has, so with the sidebar open the box you type in is no longer
  squeezed.
