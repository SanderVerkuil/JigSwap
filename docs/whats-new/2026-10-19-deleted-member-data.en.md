When a member deletes their account, their library goes with it, so Browse and
your circles only show puzzles someone can still lend, swap or sell.

## What changes

- **Their puzzles disappear** from Browse and from the circles they were shared
  in, together with their photos.
- **Circles keep going.** A circle whose owner deletes their account passes to
  the admin who has been in it longest, or else to its longest-standing member,
  who becomes an admin. Only a circle with nobody else left in it is deleted.
- **Households keep going** too: if the admin leaves this way, the
  longest-standing member takes over.
- **Open trades are cancelled, and you're told.** If you had a trade under way
  with them, it's cancelled and you get a notification saying so, with a line
  in the trade's chat.
- **Your history stays.** A puzzle they sold, swapped or gave you keeps its full
  history, and reviews and trades stay too. The member who left shows as
  **Deleted member**, without a name, picture or link.
