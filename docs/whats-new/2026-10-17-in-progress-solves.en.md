You no longer have to wait until the last piece to log a puzzle.

- **Start a solve** from any of your puzzles (or one you've borrowed) and pick
  the start date, in the past or the future.
- **Solving now** on your dashboard lists what you're working on, with a
  one-click **Finish**.
- **Completions** shows your open solves on top and your history below.
- **Share it, if you like.** Turn on _Share in-progress puzzles with friends_
  in your account Preferences and mutual followers see what you're solving on
  your profile and in their activity feed. It's off by default.
