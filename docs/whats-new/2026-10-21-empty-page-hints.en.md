When there's nothing on a page yet, it now tells you what goes there and how
to begin.

- **Collections** explains what a collection is, with **New Collection**.
- **Borrowed** explains how borrowing works and links to **Browse Puzzles**.
- **Messages** explains how a conversation starts, with **Message a member**.
- **Goals** explains what a goal is, with **New Goal**.
