A few changes to make writing messages easier.

- **Attach sits next to Send.** The attach button is now a paperclip on the
  right of the message box, just before the send button. It opens the same
  menu as before: a puzzle, photos or a location.
- **Share a puzzle in a circle chat.** In a circle's group chat you can now
  attach one of your own puzzles, such as one you've shared with the circle.
  Puzzles only some members can see (for example through your household or
  friends who follow you back) can be shared too: members who can't see them
  get a placeholder instead.
- **Formatting, links and emoji.** Write `**bold**`, `*italic*`,
  `__underline__`, `~~strikethrough~~` or `` `code` ``, or select text and
  press Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I or Ctrl+U (⌘ on a Mac). Web addresses become links, and
  on a computer the smiley button next to the message box adds an emoji. See
  [Messages](/docs/staying-informed/messages#format-your-messages).
