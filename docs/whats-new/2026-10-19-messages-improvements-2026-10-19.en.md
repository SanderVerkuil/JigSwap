A few changes to make writing messages easier.

- **Attach sits next to Send.** The attach button is now a paperclip on the
  right of the message box, just before the send button. It opens the same
  menu as before: a puzzle, photos or a location.
- **Share a puzzle in a circle chat.** In a circle's group chat you can now
  attach one of your own puzzles that everyone in the circle can see, such as
  one you've shared with the circle.
