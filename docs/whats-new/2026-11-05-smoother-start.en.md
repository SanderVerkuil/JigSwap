---

- **Quick setup** saves your picks when you press **Continue**, and asks before
  it drops anything you haven't saved. It takes you to the next step you haven't
  done, and the people step no longer needs a username.
- **Show me around** on the Quick setup card starts a short tour of JigSwap.
- **The dashboard** welcomes new members, skips rows of zeros and names the
  puzzle in each Latest row.
- **The 3D shelf** fits the whole row, and a phone shows up to four boxes.
