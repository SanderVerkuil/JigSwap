title: "Change many puzzles at once"
summary: "On My Puzzles, select several puzzles and offer them, withdraw their offers, add them to a collection or share them with a circle in one go. Search, sort and filters make a big shelf easy to go through."
category: feature
tour: change-many-puzzles
---

- **Select several.** Press **Select** next to the puzzle count, pick the
  puzzles or press **Select all**, then use the bar at the bottom of the list.
  Offers you change come with **Undo**.
- **Search, sort and filter** your puzzles. Your choices are still there when
  you open a puzzle and go back.
- **Adding a puzzle** takes you to its page, and nothing is offered until you
  choose to.
- **Private stays your choice.** A private puzzle is visible while you offer it,
  and private again once you withdraw every offer.
- **Collections:** add any of your puzzles, and delete a collection you no
  longer need. Removing a puzzle from a collection comes with **Undo**.
