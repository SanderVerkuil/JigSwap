You can now invite a friend straight from JigSwap with their email address.

- **Follow each other.** On **People → Find people**, under _Not on JigSwap
  yet?_, enter their address and choose **Invite by email**. Once they sign
  up, they're asked whether you two should follow each other.
- **Join your household.** Household admins find **Invite by email** on the
  Household page, next to the invite link.
- **One email, nothing more.** We send one invitation in your language, with
  your name. It's valid for 14 days, no reminders follow, and JigSwap keeps no
  copy of the address.

You see your invitations under **Sent invitations**, and you can **Revoke**
one while it's open. You can send up to 10 email invitations a day.
