title: "See when your puzzle image is still being checked"
summary: "If the check of an image you upload for a puzzle takes longer, JigSwap now tells you, instead of just showing a placeholder."
category: improvement
---

Every image you upload for a puzzle is checked before anyone sees it, you
included. Usually that's done before you save. When it takes longer:

- **You get a message** after saving, saying the image is still being checked.
- **Your own puzzles** show **Being checked** on the placeholder until
  the check has finished. Other members just see the placeholder.

You don't need to do anything: the image is checked again automatically.
