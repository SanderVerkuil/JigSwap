A member's profile used to show only totals. Now it lists what's behind them:

- **Their puzzles**, as cards you can open.
- **Their finished solves**, with the puzzle, the date, how long it took and
  any approved photos.

Both lists load more with **Show more**. You see them when you're signed in
and the profile is open to you: it's public, it's your own, or you follow each
other. Private notes never show.
