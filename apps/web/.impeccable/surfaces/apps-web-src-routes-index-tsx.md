## Scope

Public marketing surface: home, How it works, Features, About, Contact, legal, public header and footer; public catalogue, docs and auth pages inherit the world. Mode: Persuade (Read for docs and legal).

## Audience and action

Households and friends with shelves of boxes, Dutch first. They should believe they can finally see what everyone owns, and start a shelf. Proof on hand: original wimmelbild art, live counts, the family's own quote. Nothing else may be claimed.

## Decisions

- Chosen direction: the street with the fronts off (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the owner on 2 November 2026). Reference render: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/street.png`. Build path: code-led, no image generation in this harness.
- Taste direction: page-is-the-object, palette and type kept. The sheet's hero slot was replaced by the owner's chosen composition. Its guest-list proof slot was replaced by figures inside a sentence, because no names exist to print and the owner kept the live counts.
- Keep: live counts and the words the product uses for exchange. Do not settle trade, swap and exchange across the product.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The street owns one idea: take the fronts off and every household's shelf is on view. It refuses the centred hero with three feature cards, and it refuses a jigsaw-piece motif.

OWN-WORLD: Flat sky field, canal-house green ink, marigold action, white drawers, plank-tan ledges. Young Serif for display, Hanken Grotesk for everything read. Filled shapes with real edges and small radii; drawn gable skyline; original wimmelbild art as the only imagery.

STORY: The visitor sees people's homes with their boxes on view, understands that this is who has what, tries a floor, reads the three-step lending slip, sees each capability as one storey, and starts a shelf.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav on the sky band. The h1 runs almost the full measure in the sky, supporting sentence, marigold action, Browse link and the live count directly beneath it. The cutaway house fills the rest edge to edge, with three real floor pins; the chosen floor is struck forward and its shelf docks beside the house.

FORM: Doll's-house cutaway of a Dutch street, first on the ordered list, IMPECCABLE'S PICK. seed:e0ac332771293b33. Signature interaction: choosing a floor snaps that household's shelf forward while the others stay in view. Motion grammar: entrances once per section part, press feedback on controls, the curtain footer as the one loud moment.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
